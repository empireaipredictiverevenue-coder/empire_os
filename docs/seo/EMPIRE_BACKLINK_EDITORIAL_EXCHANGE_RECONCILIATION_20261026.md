## Backlink exchange strategy for the ONE SEO stack

The original link exchange concept should be editor-controlled, useful source collaborations, NOT reciprocal swaps or link farms. Google classifies excessive exchange or automated placement to manipulate rankings as link spam:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
Sponsored compensation should be qualified appropriately:
https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/qualify-outbound-links

1. Demand: existing GSC, Marcus Keyword Universe/semantic bridge and Jev provide specific research or buyer questions, not artificial search volumes.
2. Sources: Common Crawl and Search Fabric find historical or likely publishers; original supervised spider independently verifies current page-level evidence, observing robots/rights/rate budgets.
3. Match: existing Editorial Exchange/Marketing Growth owner checks a real publisher audience, guidelines, originality, contact suppression and existing signed content rights. Six historical records and five publisher-research rows must not be added into a false total without dedup.
4. Contribution: genuine original, attributed technical/public-data research. Expert human byline is required where a publisher requires it. The publisher independently decides acceptance and outbound links.
5. Proof: only genuinely visible third-party anchors or editorial mentions with exact source URL, target URL, observed_at and captured evidence enter original BacklinkObservation or AI citation review.
6. Measurement: distinct observations for external link, owned GSC clicks, Google/Bing/ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini citations, referred sessions, consented enquiries, accepted opportunities and realised paid revenue.
7. Learn: feed actual outcomes back through existing Jev/SEO Director. No mass link swaps, no fake placements, no paid editorial, no unapproved emails, no duplicate CRM/DB.
