## Executive finding

Google's 2026 AI optimization guidance explicitly prioritizes crawlable, indexed, useful original content and warns against generic GEO hacks, unnecessary llms.txt or artificial chunking. A July 2026 critical survey of 45 GEO studies finds cross-platform generic manipulation unreliable, while an October 2026 empirical paper with 158,099 answers and 468,037 citations finds meaningfully matching AI-retrieval subqueries is more explanatory than mechanical on-page markup. Therefore: make current Empire Search Fabric + semantic graph + Jev identify grounded intent clusters and improve original pages, then measure external citations and enquiries separately.

1. DISCOVER: existing GSC final rows, GSC new Generative AI impressions (UI/export first), Bing Webmaster citation export, Search Fabric, owned site crawl, historical Common Crawl, manual reviewed AI-answer samples and independently verified backlinks.
2. VALIDATE: annotate engine/surface, exact URL, query or prompt, locale, timestamp, source receipt, provenance, model/session if known, legal/robots/terms, freshness and evidence state; suppress duplicates and synthetic/untrusted evidence.
3. UNDERSTAND: use existing Keyword Universe + semantic topic graph to cluster real buyer questions and query fan-outs (cost, alternatives, proof, comparison, geography, requirements, vendor choice). Keep fan-out hypotheses distinct from provider-observed queries.
4. PRIORITISE: existing Jev typed decisions plus deterministic evidence score (intent fit, company/territory, page readiness, source quality, achievable editorial value, buyer-payer relevance, expected cost/time, uncertainty). Missing evidence yields hold, not made-up score.
5. IMPROVE: create substantial, sourced answers inside CURRENT pillar and industry pages; differentiated methods, samples, dated charts, definitions, evidence limitations, comparisons, valid authorship, natural contextual links, supporting original visuals/video; editorial and website owner approve publish.
6. DISTRIBUTE: no paid promotion; earn genuine mentions from relevant publishers, associations and YouTube; avoid link schemes, fake review/testimonials, planted prompt instructions, mass doorway city pages or scrape-and-republish.
7. MEASURE: Search Console organic query/page metrics, Google AI impressions only from appropriate native report, Bing citation export, externally captured ChatGPT/Perplexity/Gemini answers, referral UTM/source evidence, verified opted-in enquiries and EmpireDB settled revenue independently.
8. LEARN: compare matched pre/post date windows, control unchanged pages, repeated prompts, paraphrases/locale, real publisher-release timestamps. Jev may propose next changes only using reviewed labels, not self-confirmed outcomes.
