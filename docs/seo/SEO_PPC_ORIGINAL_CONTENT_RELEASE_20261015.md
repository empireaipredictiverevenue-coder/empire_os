## P1 draft addition for `/industries/roofing`

### Where a roofing enquiry becomes a commercial opportunity

A search for roof replacement is not the same as a qualified roofing customer. The homeowner may want a repair, live outside a contractor's coverage, be comparing future options or need a service the buyer does not perform. Treating every visit or call as payable disguises the economics and creates poor experiences for both sides.

Empire AI is building the evidence layer that separates those outcomes. We start with a stated source—such as an Empire-owned organic-search page—and describe what the customer actually requested. The next checks are geographic eligibility, job type, buyer availability, the consumer's permission for the intended contact, and the agreed definition of a billable event.

### The five checks that matter before routing a roofing call

**1. Source and intent.** Was the request initiated by a homeowner who knows what they are asking for? Can the source page, local context and disclosures be identified? A Google search impression is awareness; an organic click is a site visit; neither proves a booked job.

**2. Job eligibility.** Roof replacement, installation, emergency repair, leak inspection and storm-restoration work are different service types. A buyer may accept only some of them, so categorisation must follow the buyer's written eligibility rules rather than a broad keyword label.

**3. Territory and capacity.** A ZIP code alone is not a buyer agreement. Confirm currently accepted markets, dispatch hours, job categories, capacity limits and exclusions. If a contractor is not accepting the relevant job or territory, the enquiry should not be sold as accepted inventory.

**4. Consent and routing.** Before routing a consumer, agree who controls any call recording, what disclosures are required, whether original caller identity is passed on, and how duplicate, abandoned or misrouted calls will be treated. Exact rules can vary by location; these controls require legal and partner-specific review.

**5. The economic outcome.** A ringing phone is not automatically revenue. Compare the event reported by the call system with the buyer's accepted-event definition, its returns and dispute window, the contractually payable amount and the actual settlement. Keep projected value separate from independently reconciled proceeds.

### What Empire AI helps operators see

The wider Predictive Revenue platform combines territory, weather, property, permit, business and search signals for commercial review. It can organise the evidence behind a proposed roofing opportunity and help teams decide what to verify next. A forecast or candidate lead is never presented as a fulfilled sale just because a model scored it highly.

For roofers and network buyers, the useful question isn't *"How many leads do you have?"* It's *"Which source and service types fit a real accepting buyer, in which territories, under which measurable terms?"*

**Suggested existing-site CTA:** *Discuss a scoped roofing territory or buyer-corridor review* → `/commercial`. Use the existing nonbinding proposal path; do not claim approved inbound call inventory or turn on the homeowner `/roofing` call route.

#### Roofing page FAQs (visible content before any schema)

**Are organic roofing website clicks the same as pay-per-call events?** No. An organic click means a search visitor reached the page. A pay-per-call event depends on the particular buyer agreement and the provider-side qualifying call result.

**Can roofing enquiries from any US ZIP be monetised?** Only where the actual partner has agreed to accept the service and source in that ZIP, during approved hours, subject to consent and quality rules. Nationwide marketing copy is not independent evidence of nationwide buyer capacity.

**Does Empire AI guarantee contractor appointments?** No. Candidate opportunities and forecasts must remain distinguishable from verified appointments, accepted commercial events and collected revenue.

## P1 draft addition for `/industries/solar`

### Solar demand is local. Payable referrals are contractual.

Solar search traffic can indicate interest in panels, home energy costs, batteries or installer quotes, but it does not prove that a homeowner is eligible for an installer programme or that an advertiser will pay for a click. A credible solar acquisition programme starts with a clear user need and a specific route to an accepting provider.

**Verify the geography.** In the UK, a solar programme may require postcode coverage and installer qualifications such as MCS-related requirements. In the US, states, ZIPs, utility territory and installer capacity may matter. Australian publisher arrangements can be state-specific and may pay for an accepted lead, not simply a website visit. The actual partner must confirm the current offer and legal eligibility; one country's terms should never be copied into another.

**Separate the commercial events.** A visitor clicking a solar article is a search engagement. A submitted form is a consent-sensitive lead. A confirmed consultation is an appointment. A signed installation is a different outcome again. These events must not be counted interchangeably, and quoted payouts require the governing programme documentation.

**Protect consumer choice.** If a page eventually recommends a paid partner, disclose the relationship clearly near the recommendation, use the appropriate sponsored-link attribute, explain the intended data transfer and avoid pre-checked or ambiguous consent. Do not collect details until an approved destination and retention process exist.

**Make attribution auditable.** An accepted solar enquiry must remain tied to its actual source, programme, consent, geographic eligibility and buyer acceptance. Reversals, installation disputes and delayed payment should be visible before a publisher commission is presented as realised revenue.

**Suggested existing-site CTA:** *Review solar territory, installer coverage and commercial evidence* → `/commercial`. Don't place an affiliate clickout or consumer form on this page until a real programme and destination are approved.

#### Solar page FAQs

**Do you get paid whenever someone clicks a solar link?** Not by default. Pay-per-click requires a contract expressly paying for accepted clicks. Many public solar affiliate programmes instead compensate accepted leads or completed outcomes.

**Can UK and Australian solar leads go to the same buyers?** Not automatically. Each programme needs independently confirmed territory, consent, data-sharing, installer and settlement terms.

## P2 draft addition for `/predictive-revenue`

### Revenue intelligence is only predictive until the outcome is verified

Most dashboards can show a pipeline value, a search trend or a confidence score. None of those numbers is money in the bank. A useful Predictive Revenue system shows how a signal becomes a decision, then checks the decision against what actually happened.

The operating chain is straightforward: observe a documented market signal, identify a plausible opportunity, establish whether a real buyer or customer can act on it, present an approved offer, record accepted activity, fulfil the agreement and reconcile the economic result. Each stage has a different standard of evidence. Missing buyer terms, unsigned agreements and unverified events remain visible as missing—not silently converted into forecast revenue.

This distinction matters to an SMB estimating next month's sales as much as to an enterprise allocating capacity across markets. Leaders need to know **what is supported, what is forecast, what is blocked, and what changed after the outcome**.

Empire AI's platform is being built to support that evidence loop across revenue, CRM, search, customer intent and markets. Some integrations and individual customer deployments require separate verification; a product demonstration is not a claim of universal live execution.

**Suggested CTA:** retain the existing *Request Deployment* form with its narrow, explicitly consented email follow-up; do not treat submitting it as a signed contract or completed payment.
