Empire AI develops Predictive Revenue intelligence software and offers founder-led consultancy discussions for businesses evaluating where AI, commercial data and better evidence could improve decisions. Our work is centred on distinguishing early signals and forecasts from actual enquiries, accepted commercial outcomes, payments and realised revenue.

## What businesses can enquire about

**Opportunity Assessment — from £2,500 plus applicable VAT.** For an organisation with a specific business problem, the first step is to describe the decision it needs to make, the processes and systems involved, and what a useful assessment would need to establish. The published starting price is approved, but a specific statement of work, deliverables, timetable and final terms still need founder review.

**Strategy Blueprint — from £7,500 plus applicable VAT.** Businesses considering a larger AI or revenue-intelligence programme can request a discussion about an appropriately scoped strategy engagement. The scope and delivery arrangements are not pre-agreed in the public catalogue.

**Strategic Advisory — from £3,000 per month plus applicable VAT.** Organisations evaluating ongoing strategic support can request proposed terms and fit. No advisory availability or outcome is implied merely by the public catalogue entry.

Our published commercial offering also lists separate research/intelligence products: Competitor Search Gap, Search Opportunity Map, Technical Search Audit and Solar Opportunity Map — US. Those are different product lines from the consultancy options. Delivery and payment eligibility must be checked for each product rather than assumed from a listed price.

## How a commercial enquiry works

A prospective customer can use our existing commercial enquiry form to describe its company, industry, geography, systems and business goal. This **requests a founder-reviewed, nonbinding proposal**. It does not submit a paid order, enrol anyone in unrelated marketing, automatically initiate a deployment or guarantee a result.

Predictive Revenue software is a broader product direction under development. We do not claim that every capability shown on the website is immediately deployable or that modelling alone creates income.

A serious proposal needs to identify the customer decision, available evidence, required permissions, intended deliverables, exclusions, human review, acceptance criteria, cost and payment terms. We should say **unknown** when we cannot substantiate a claim.
