## Executive finding

The problem is **not** that EmpireOS has only one SEO agent. Production already registers `marketing_growth`, has a marketing department blueprint dated September 20, specialist roles, Search Intelligence, Marketing Agency, Analytics, Conversion, Content/Media and an existing department-cycle timer. A 16 October read-only call to `observe_marketing_agency('/srv/empire_os')` returned: five agency jobs, five campaign plans, four tagged `predictive_revenue_enterprise` and one `general_contractor`, twenty proposed content assets, thirty proposed creative briefs, five proposed conversion plans and five attribution plans. It reported stale `competitor` and `search` evidence, all five revenue predictions `UNAVAILABLE` and **zero external spend / zero execution authority**. The first-party Search Intelligence summary returned zero stored search opportunities and zero recognized search revenue.

**Diagnosis:** a real department exists as architecture and observe-only planning, but the **specialist workflow, balanced campaign backlog, fresh evidence, controlled content release and commercial feedback loop** are not demonstrated end to end. Launching a second Agent OS would duplicate that infrastructure while failing to close these gaps. This plan instead formalizes eight existing specialist desks with one review queue and shared revenue scorecard.

## Deep-dive: differentiated source-driven content campaigns

**Research approach:** official datasets plus buyer decision framework, not copying articles or pretending data proves individual purchase intent.

- **UK AI audits / enterprise consulting:** The actual government [AI Adoption Research](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-adoption-research) contains UK business adoption barriers and published data tables. Competitors already publish detailed [AI audit pricing benchmarks](https://www.augustova.co.uk/guides/ai-readiness-audit-uk-sme) and [their own £2,500 entry-level services](https://www.augustova.co.uk/ai-consultancy). Our differentiator must be honest measurement/proof, transparent scope, run cost, consent and risk—not "AI readiness 2026" generic fluff. Current `/commercial` explains founder-priced £2,500 Assessment; confirm deliverable fulfilment and GSC indexing before a content update.
- **Roofing US:** NOAA [Storm Events](https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/storm-events-database/) exposes searchable/bulk historical storm records; credible for public territory context, *not* proof a house requires repairs or that a buyer will pay. Build a clearly sourced, date-stamped storm-event methodology and separate accepted/payable call explanations. The external Lead Smart buyer route, ANI, buyer leg and accepted paid-event evidence are not verified; do not claim real payout, active live campaigns or qualified leads.
- **Solar US and UK:** NLR's [PVWatts V8](https://developer.nlr.gov/docs/solar/pvwatts/v8/) gives electricity yield estimates, not lead volume or purchase intent. UK's [Planning Data API](https://www.data.gov.uk/collections/land-and-property/planning-data) offers structured public planning/housing data with incomplete coverage caveats. Combine only where the sources actually apply and keep territory/consent/referral payout terms visibly unknown until accepted.
- **Property UK:** The UK Planning Data platform reports over 100 datasets with API access and explicit completeness limits. A documented case-based planning-constraints audit may be cited by trade publications and open-data communities; no personalised planning guarantee.
- **Dental / healthcare US:** [CMS provider data](https://data.cms.gov/provider-data/datasets) is suitable for narrowly scoped public provider facts, but does not prove private dental patients, future patient demand, booking intent or consent. Keep programme on hold until buyer/rights/medical advertising review.
- **Legal / mass tort US:** [Judicial Panel on Multidistrict Litigation statistics](https://www.jpml.uscourts.gov/mdl-quarterly-caseload-summary) can support careful public litigation process research, not settlement forecasts, direct claimant targeting or attorney referral claims. High compliance risk and missing commercial evidence: watch, don't launch.

## The actual department, not another generic agent

**Astra executive / founder priority** → **existing Marketing Director (`marketing_growth`)** → *eight specialist desks under one campaign register*:

1. **Strategy / Portfolio:** selects ICP and real monetization lane, prevents duplicate "campaigns" around the same vague topic; owns stop/go and commercial priority.
2. **Demand / Competitor intelligence:** GSC organic and generative evidence, query/URL, SERP snapshot and source-proven competitors, Google Trends, first-party buyer needs; owns research quality.
3. **Technical SEO / Indexing:** SEO health, canonicals, sitemap, signed AEO pages, owned site legal/privacy and internal navigation. Existing gateway/site owners control release; this desk only reports.
4. **Original Editorial / Utility Research:** real data, reproducible methodology, sources, calculators that actually compute and show assumptions, qualified copy; no fake features or revenue cases.
5. **AEO/GEO/AI citation analyst:** Google AI report, Bing AI Performance, first-party Bing/Copilot references, ChatGPT search crawlability, query-specific answer usefulness; verifies the *different* metrics separately.
6. **Earned Distribution / Partnerships:** trade editorial, useful industry communities, journalist quotes, newsletters, qualified partner co-marketing and real endorsements; suppression/approval before each send.
7. **Conversion / Commercial Intelligence:** pages and CTAs, consent, legitimate enquiry, accepted downstream buyer economics, CRM and financial proof; never substitutes session counts for payments.
8. **Trust / Compliance / Brand QA:** approves source licensing, safety, legal/regulatory consistency, owner brand, original claims, accessibility and content value. Prevents publishing into ASTRA's unfinished website.

## Operator campaign process (enforced by Control Fabric/owners)

`IDEA → SOURCE_CHECK → SEARCH_DEMAND/SERP_REVIEW → ICP+OFFER/BILLABLE_EVENT → ORIGINAL_ASSET → TECH/PRIVACY/BRAND QA → FOUNDER+SITE_OWNER APPROVAL → PUBLISH ON EXISTING VERIFIED ROUTE → GSC/BING/AI OBSERVATION → APPROVED EARNED DISTRIBUTION → CONSENTED ENQUIRY → VERIFIED BUYER/OFFER → PAYMENT/FULFILMENT → REALIZED GROSS PROFIT → LEARNING`

**Business handoff:** Marketing Director supplies the campaign and scope; Search Intelligence establishes observed demand; Content OS produces original material, not generic slop; ASTRA website owner decides when/where to integrate only on finished/owned surfaces; Traffic & SEO Director conducts indexability and internal link checks; Distribution queues pitches/drafts for founder approval; Revenue CRM reconciles real identities and payment/fulfilment. All of this uses the existing department cycle and existing canonical data, never a second background daemon.

Every campaign packet must include: exact buyer, problem, offer and economics, source timestamp and licence, target query and actual Google/Bing results with locale, existing page or reviewed new-product asset need, owner, human expertise claim checker, forecast marked unknown if evidence missing, traffic/citation measurement plan, privacy/consent, publication/marketing approval, stop conditions, and attribution to verified revenue.

## First 24 hours: do useful work rather than promise #1 rankings

1. Marketing Director picks **one** first experiment: UK AI Assessment commercial buyer guide, or the verified Google/Bing AI-citation methodology if there is an easier path to an original research resource. The founder's £2,500 offer already has a published URL; site ownership still controls changes.
2. Demand desk captures current actual UK/US localized SERPs for 6–10 very specific search questions; records exact date, real competing pages, search volume **unknown unless observed**, official sources and buyer intent. Do not extrapolate screenshots to rankings.
3. Editorial desk gathers primary sources (UK AI Adoption Research tables, official Google/Bing 2026 AI measurement documentation, owner-authorized Empire source data) and writes a noncommodity scope/value methodology and FAQ draft with no fake case studies.
4. Technical desk checks `/commercial` and Google/Bing indexability. GSC indexed-version 16 Oct showed commercial `URL unknown to Google` and homepage indexed Oct 12; be patient during normal crawl delay and use permitted Search Console verification. Do not spam indexing submissions or use Google's restricted job/livestream Indexing API.
5. Conversion desk checks actual consent, privacy notice, offer, callback/email process, qualification, pricing and fulfillment. Prospective £2,500 assessment **is not** a recorded sale until signed/paid according to policy.
6. Earned distribution desk assembles 3–5 *relevant* editor/source opportunities, including no-cost HARO/Featured-style requests where a real subject expert can answer; no automatic pitches or unsanctioned posting.
7. Research Council reports **only verifiable outputs:** measured rank check if reliable, indexing state, actual GSC query impressions, clicks, consented responses and realized payments separately. A 24-hour cycle can complete a quality brief; Google indexing can take days/weeks.
