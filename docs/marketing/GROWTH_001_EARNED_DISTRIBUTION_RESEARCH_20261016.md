## Original editorial angle — for the human founder or verified expert to develop

**Question:** What must a UK business verify before spending money on AI adoption or authorising an autonomous agent?

Explore the demonstrable differences between an observable event, a model forecast, a consented customer enquiry, an accepted transaction and verified paid outcomes. Draw on Empire's real first-party system control boundaries and official UK adoption statistics; do NOT invent an enterprise client, claimed benefit, implemented consultancy deliverable or independently published case study. Compare DSIT 2025 fieldwork and ONS September 2026 AI adoption as distinct surveys, not a directly comparable trend.

**Suggested independent value:** Specify the business baseline and possible no-AI alternative, identify responsibilities and source permissions, define measurable acceptance tests and failure-handling, explain when to stop a proposed AI implementation. A human expert must personally substantiate the perspective and write or materially author the contribution, especially for publications whose rules exclude AI-authored work.

**Editorial versus sales separation:** Empire's existing commercial page supports a founder-reviewed nonbinding proposal. Independent editorials must follow publisher policies, avoid disguised product CTAs or mass-syndication, and may carry no outbound link at all. An editorial link or brand mention becomes a verified acquisition signal only after it visibly exists at a real third-party page URL.

## Operating workflow

1. Distribution desk confirms publication eligibility, relevance, restrictions, possible fees and whether a genuine editor is currently accepting submissions. Do not infer 'free' merely because a contact address is public.
2. Founder/expert provides an actual first-hand technical insight and checks every claim before authorship. No invented customer outcomes or borrowed copy.
3. Marketing Director and compliance desk review sources, rights, nonpromotional framing and brand use.
4. Founder explicitly approves the named pitch or editorial submission. No automated emails, posting, syndication, fees or contacts before approval.
5. Measure only authentic published URLs, actual referral sessions and individually verified consented enquiries. Referral sessions are not Google organic clicks and are not proven paid customers.

**Execution:** NONE. No email sent, article placed, backlink received, account created, fees spent or revenue generated. These are research opportunities only.
