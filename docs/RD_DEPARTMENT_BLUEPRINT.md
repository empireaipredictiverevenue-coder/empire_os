# Empire AI — Research & Development Department Blueprint

Date: 2026-09-20
Status: CANONICAL R&D OPERATING MODEL

R&D converts scientific/technical/commercial uncertainty into validated
capabilities, defensible IP and product advantages.

It is not a collection of experimental agents.

## 1. Mission

**Discover -> investigate -> hypothesize -> prototype -> evaluate -> validate ->
transfer -> productize -> measure commercial impact -> learn.**

## 4. Research Portfolio

Portfolio buckets:

### Horizon 1 — Core Improvement
0–3 months
- reliability
- cost
- conversion
- forecast quality
- source quality
- workflow improvements

### Horizon 2 — Adjacent Products
3–12 months
- Permit Intelligence
- Satellite/Storm
- Buyer Intelligence
- new market/data products
- new agent capabilities
- advanced Quant products

### Horizon 3 — Frontier
12+ months
- advanced commercial world models
- stronger general agentic intelligence
- autonomous research
- novel multimodal/causal methods
- new computational paradigms
- quantum/hybrid optimization research only if a real problem justifies it

Do not sacrifice H1/H2 commercial value for frontier theater.

## 6. Research Lifecycle

IDEA
-> TRIAGE
-> RESEARCH
-> HYPOTHESIS
-> PROTOTYPE
-> EVALUATION
-> SHADOW
-> VALIDATED
-> TRANSFER_READY
-> PRODUCT/ENGINEERING
-> COMMERCIAL OUTCOME

Failed hypotheses are valid R&D outcomes.

## 7. Research Quality

Require:
- provenance/citations
- reproducibility
- baseline
- metrics
- uncertainty
- limitations
- benchmark
- point-in-time correctness
- seeds/configs when stochastic
- versioned data/model/code
- no synthetic-as-real outcome claims

## 9. R&D + Quant Brain

Quant Brain supplies:
- uncertainty
- VOI
- experiment analysis
- power/sample reasoning later
- risk-adjusted research priority
- benchmark comparisons

Research with high uncertainty but high VOI may outrank obvious incremental work.

## 10. R&D + AGI

Agentic research system may:
- discover papers/tools
- synthesize evidence
- generate hypotheses
- propose experiments
- write prototypes
- create test cases
- run local evaluations

It may not:
- self-certify success
- deploy itself to production
- widen its permissions
- treat synthetic tests as commercial proof

## 13. R&D Control Rules

- Research != production.
- Prototype != product.
- Simulation != outcome.
- Benchmark != customer value.
- Model accuracy != revenue.
- Agent autonomy != intelligence.
- Research promotion requires independent evaluation.
- High-risk experiments remain sandboxed.
- Production changes follow normal governance.

## 15. R&D Success

R&D wins when it produces:
- better predictions
- stronger data/intelligence
- lower cost
- better products
- new revenue
- defensible capabilities
- reusable knowledge
- validated negative results

The goal is not "more research."
The goal is **compounding technical and commercial advantage.**
