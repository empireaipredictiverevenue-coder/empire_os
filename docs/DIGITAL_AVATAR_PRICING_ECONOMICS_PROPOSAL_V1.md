## Why this is not avatar-API pricing

Public market anchors observed 2026-10-02:

- Tavus developer plans publicly list roughly $59/month for 100 conversational
  video minutes, $397/month for roughly 1,250 minutes, $975/month for about
  4,000 minutes, with Enterprise custom.
- HeyGen LiveAvatar publicly lists about $99/month for roughly 550–1,100
  streaming minutes and $475/month for roughly 3,000–6,000 minutes depending on
  integration mode; Enterprise is custom.
- Recall publicly lists Meeting Bot recording at $0.50/hour, with built-in
  transcription at $0.15/hour.
- AI-digital-worker market references in 2026 place published autonomous sales
  agents from hundreds to several thousand dollars per month, with some
  enterprise AI-worker products starting around several thousand per month.

Those are infrastructure / point-solution anchors. Empire packages a broader
commercial system: Clone identity + meeting attendance + grounded evidence +
Predictive Revenue + specialist agents + routing + continuity + governed
authority + commercial/learning loop.

The pricing proposal therefore uses value positioning rather than marking up
avatar-rendering minutes.

## Proposed entitlement

### Copilot

Designed for founder/AE augmentation.

- 1 active authorised clone
- SHADOW + COPILOT
- 40 verified completed meetings/month
- target concurrency entitlement: 1
- grounded meeting intelligence
- specialist consultation
- private continuity memory
- Predictive Revenue refresh/recommendations
- $35 per verified-meeting overage
- proposed $1,500 implementation fee

### Workforce

Designed for a revenue team deploying several specialist clones.

- 5 active authorised clones
- SHADOW + COPILOT
- 150 verified completed meetings/month
- target concurrency entitlement: 3
- clone-role routing
- capability + availability assignment
- specialist consultation
- continuity memory
- verified usage/quota review
- $30 per verified-meeting overage
- proposed $5,000 implementation fee

### White-Label Workforce

Designed for agencies, software companies and larger operators deploying
Empire-powered AI workforces under their own brand.

- 15 active authorised clones
- SHADOW + COPILOT
- 400 verified completed meetings/month
- target concurrency entitlement: 10
- white-label workforce projection
- clone-role templates
- tenant-scoped identity/media isolation
- routing + concurrency + continuity
- specialist consultation
- verified usage/quota review
- mandatory AI disclosure remains non-removable
- $25 per verified-meeting overage
- proposed $15,000 implementation fee

## Economics policy

Target policy margin: 75%.
Minimum permitted gross contribution policy: 70%.

Until real cost evidence exists, the proposal uses policy ceilings, not claimed
actual cost:

| Package | Monthly price | Acquisition-cost ceiling | Fulfilment-cost ceiling | Policy contribution | Policy margin |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Copilot | $1,500 | $150 | $225 | $1,125 | 75% |
| Workforce | $5,000 | $500 | $750 | $3,750 | 75% |
| White-Label | $12,500 | $1,250 | $1,875 | $9,375 | 75% |

These ceilings are commercial guardrails. They are explicitly NOT observations
of actual CAC, GPU cost, inference cost or support cost.

## Overage economics

Meeting overage is deliberately above raw provider infrastructure pricing
because the unit being sold is a governed AI revenue meeting, not a meeting-bot
recording minute.

However, commercial activation of per-meeting overage is blocked until Empire
has evidence for:
- observed meeting-duration distribution;
- observed inference/TTS/render cost;
- observed Recall/provider cost where applicable;
- support/ops allocation;
- peak concurrency capacity.

If external real-time video rendering remains in the fulfilment path, duration
risk must be measured before treating a flat per-meeting overage as VERIFIED.

## Implementation fee

The proposed implementation fee covers setup work such as:
- authorised identity/consent onboarding;
- clone profile preparation;
- company/product intelligence binding;
- role/workforce configuration;
- evidence and authority setup;
- integration validation;
- controlled launch verification.

Implementation fees require their own catalog/product or governed commercial
term before they can be charged. They are not activated by this proposal.

## Enterprise expansion

Proposed quote-led floor: $15,000/month recurring when the customer requires
one or more of:
- more than 15 active clones;
- more than 400 verified meetings/month;
- more than 10 proposed concurrent meetings;
- bespoke private deployment;
- custom compliance/SLA;
- dedicated specialist/runtime capacity;
- materially custom product/integration work.

The $15,000 floor is a negotiation policy proposal, not an active product row.

## Required evidence before catalog activation

Price approval alone is insufficient.

Still required:
1. Founder approval of exact monthly prices, implementation fees, included
   units, overage, role limits and concurrency targets.
2. Verified acquisition-cost basis or governed ceiling evidence.
3. Verified fulfilment-cost basis from actual runtime/provider/compute/support
   evidence.
4. Verified margin policy.
5. Capacity benchmark proving proposed concurrency entitlements.
6. Duration/cost benchmark supporting flat verified-meeting overage.
7. Governed tenant entitlement terms.
8. Canonical product versions proposed in EmpireDB.
9. Independent catalog verification before binding_terms_ready.
10. Separate checkout/payment activation.

## Approval boundary

Approval of this proposal should authorize the exact pricing policy and permit
the next governed step: create/propose canonical Digital Avatar product
identities/versions with founder-approved PRICE basis while leaving costs,
margin verification and binding terms gated until their evidence is complete.

It must NOT by itself:
- mark catalog versions VERIFIED;
- set binding_terms_ready;
- create checkout;
- create a payment request;
- move funds;
- recognize revenue;
- authorize live meetings or Avatar speech.
