# EmpireOS | How major US lead & pay-per-call operators acquire calls
Research completed: 27 October 2026 | Original competitor intelligence owner | OBSERVE / zero-paid-spend

## Answer
Companies at scale run two complementary acquisition operations: (1) contract advertiser/buyer coverage and payout eligibility; (2) generate first-party and publisher consumer traffic. Search (SEO+PPC), affiliates, content/owned sites, paid social/native/display, selective offline/retail and call-centre follow-up all play roles. On receipt, an inquiry can become a tracked inbound call, callback, live prequalified transfer, form lead, or other contract-defined outcome. A connected call is not yet proof of an accepted payable event, and a theoretical listed payout is not earned revenue.

Its cumulative call counter is company-reported; not independent evidence of roofing calls or profitability. Sources: https://www.marketcall.com/affiliates and https://www.marketcall.com/

## What the evidence establishes
1. **Acquisition source:** owned websites, organic search, paid search, publisher partnerships, social, display/native or offline.
2. **Consumer conversion:** page view → request → explicitly consented call choice, form or transferred inquiry.
3. **Qualification:** geography, service requested, buyer opening hours, caller eligibility, duplicate checks, consent/recording, and contractual duration/intent rules.
4. **Routing and monetisation:** approved buyer capacity and price; direct delivery or multi-buyer RTB only if contractually permitted; actual accepted event and later settlement.
5. **Optimization:** source-level connected and accepted conversion rates, true acquisition/operating cost, reversals, satisfaction and buyer outcomes.

## Core distinctions
- CPC/PPC is an *acquisition cost* to the media buyer. CPL/PPL, CPA and pay-per-call are possible *payout models* on the other side of the transaction.
- Public website claims and company-wide marketing spend do not reveal which specific keyword, paid media, publisher or page yields verified phone calls.
- Ringba RTB is a monetisation/routing technique, **not** a homeowner traffic source: https://support.ringba.com/hc/en-us/articles/33203751300503-Ringba-Glossary
- Google Ads can attribute paid call conversions, but conversion-by-duration is not final accepted payout: https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6100664/about-phone-call-conversion-tracking?hl=en-GB
- Google Local Services Ads prohibits reselling leads or routing them to unrelated businesses: https://support.google.com/localservices/answer/6245891?hl=en
- Low-value, doorway-style city/ZIP microsites violate search quality policies: https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies
