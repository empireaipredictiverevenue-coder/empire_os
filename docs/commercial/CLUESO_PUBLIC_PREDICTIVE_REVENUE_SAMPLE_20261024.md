## Three-stage evidence model — free sample

| Decision | Raw signals to keep separate | Proof necessary before forecasting |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Who created account-level demand? | PLG signup, inbound enquiry, outbound discovery | First observed touch time, unique company identity, eligible source definitions and deduplication |
| 2. What became a qualified enterprise evaluation? | Product usage, first conversation, technical/commercial evaluation | Named buyer role, accepted business problem, source-to-opportunity linkage, actual evaluation start date |
| 3. What became a contracted and activated customer? | Signed contract, paid ARR/ACV, deployment/onboarding, later expansion | Signed dates and terms, real payment/recognition evidence and activation timestamp; no double counting upgrades |

### A small defensible test

Compare one **actual** past quarter of enterprise opportunities by first qualified source and latest assisting source. Start with mutually exclusive source labels to avoid allocating the same account twice to PLG and outbound; distinguish initiated, accepted and verified economic events. For a 50% outbound *target* (from the role), report actual distribution only if the company voluntarily supplies its definitions and count evidence. There is no legitimate public-data basis here for an ROI estimate, a forecast accuracy score, a pipeline value, or a claim of wasted sales spend.
