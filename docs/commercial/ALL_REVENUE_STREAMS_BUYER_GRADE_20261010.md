## Uniform commercial standard for every stream

**Identify real counterparty → establish current demand → select exact price/product/corridor → collect all missing information once → independently verify legal/compliance and delivery contract → controlled end-to-end test → founder-approved commercial steps → verified payment → fulfilled accepted outcome → reconciled realised revenue.**

The following six groups are in `PORTFOLIO_SINGLE_REQUEST_GROUPS` and included **on all 14 portfolio stream rows**, so agents can reuse the original buyer onboarding system instead of creating parallel tools:

1. **Business authority:** current legal entity, named authorising person or verified public business contact, whether they are a purchaser, reseller, insured's agent, network recruiter or potential contractor
2. **Demand and geography:** exact product needed or programme/vertical, approved territory/ZIPs, capacity, eligibility, accepted outcomes, exclusions and demand evidence
3. **Pricing and terms:** approved product code, unit, currency, MRR versus fixed fee versus qualified call, variable payout basis, duplicates/disputes, invoice dates, payout timing, duration and renewal
4. **Legal and provenance:** source rights, contractor/insurance licensing, advertising/referral rules, consent/disclosures, permitted use of personal data and special-sector controls
5. **Delivery and tests:** one accepted pilot, data or call format, tracking/source IDs, API/CRM integration, test and rollback plan, support contact, who approves deliverables and outcome measurement
6. **Onboarding and settlement:** actual contract/order form, entity and tax forms, authorised approval channel, payment method, financial reconciliation, fulfilment and retention owner

**Sent email ≠ agreement. Application page ≠ approved vendor. Catalog-ready ≠ customer-ready. 1,057 seat records ≠ 1,057 sellable seats. Payment request ≠ settled money.**

## Separate insurance workstreams — critical truth boundary

The insurance work is **three businesses**, not one: (a) sell ordinary contractor-growth or market intelligence software to licensed businesses; (b) qualify insurer/TPA/network vendors and their actual supplier onboarding standards; (c) any claim-adjustment or settlement-proceeds/percentage model, which must remain blocked until licensed/legal review. *Never* use a generic subscription to disguise regulated claims referral/adjusting compensation.

**Grounded network research starting points, not active Empire AI buyers:**

- Sedgwick Repair Solutions network: https://www.sedgwick.com/repair-solutions/managed-repair-network/ . Their contractor intake asks for company/service locations, commercial premises, trade type, software, owners, years in business and other programme memberships. This is a contractor recruitment path, **not** a verified procurement opportunity for our software.
- Sedgwick vendor program: https://www.sedgwick.com/en-gb/vendor-network/ . A submitted application does not mean approval, available assignments or purchase; the site says so.
- Contractor Connection programme: https://www.contractorconnection.com/for-contractors/ — independently recheck current requirements and geography before classifying or proposing collaboration.
- Official regulatory reference for Texas: https://tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/in/htm/in.4102.htm . For Florida 2026: https://www.flsenate.gov/Laws/Statutes/2026/626.854 . Legal counsel must interpret applicability to each actual activity; do not infer fee rights from published maximums.

**One-pass insurance network discovery brief (after a verified business counterpart is established):** which contracting party and programme role are relevant, states and trades covered, contractor registration/licensing/insurance/certification/estimating-system requirements, actual programme capacity and invitation/application decision process, whether the organisation genuinely procures independent software/data intelligence, source/privacy rules, qualification/assignment standards, pricing or software purchasing pathway, acceptance tests, payment terms, and authorised legal/technical contacts.
