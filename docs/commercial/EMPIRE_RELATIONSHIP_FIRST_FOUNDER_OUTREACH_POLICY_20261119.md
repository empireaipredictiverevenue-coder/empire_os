# Empire AI — Mandatory relationship-first outreach

**Founder directive effective 19 November 2026.** Applies to every existing EmpireOS outreach agent, business-contact workflow and related owner. This policy creates **no** new sending, CRM, financial, tenant or campaign authority.

## Objective and operating sequence

Build genuine professional relationships that produce useful conversations, qualified customers and independently verifiable revenue. Do not optimise for the number of emails sent.

**Research → Relevance → Value → Conversation → Trust → Opportunity → Commercial discussion.**

### Before any new cold introduction

1. Verify the real company, relevant department and responsible contact through reliable first-party evidence. Never fabricate people, email addresses, roles, problems or buying interest.
2. Record a specific source-backed observation and explain why it matters to that exact recipient. A plausible link or AI-generated account score is not proof.
3. Check original Smart CRM relationships, prior Gmail messages across approved mailboxes, earlier introductions, suppressions, opt-outs, invalid destinations and bounces.
4. Where identity, source, existing-contact status, recipient permission or canonical suppression is missing, **HOLD** and continue research rather than invent personalisation.
5. Never send duplicate introductions or repeatedly chase a company that has not replied.

### First email

Write naturally as a founder or a knowledgeable professional. Provide **one genuinely useful observation**, with its verified context, in short, clearly separated paragraphs. Prefer **70–120 words**, excluding signature and mandatory business details. Ask **at most one non-commercial question**. Include the correct verified sender, company and contact information, and easy opt-out.

Do **not** include pricing, product pitches, paid pilots, demo requests, calendar links, meeting bookings, unsupported performance or savings claims, generic AI descriptions, artificial urgency or fake familiarity. Replacing only the company name in a template is insufficient.

A passing copy review is **never** permission to send.

### Genuine replies and qualification

Read the authentic provider thread and respond to what the person actually said. Provide useful information before discussing a product. Do not treat polite acknowledgment, email opens, unverified reply classifications or synthetic conversations as qualified opportunities.

Only the original Sales Director and Smart CRM qualification process can assess real commercial interest. A qualified enquiry is not a paid sale; an outbound sent event is not confirmed delivery.

### Existing owner boundaries

- Search Fabric and Buyer Scout own verified company, person, industry and first-party research.
- Smart CRM and approved Gmail reconciliation own prior contact, suppression, opt-outs, duplication and relationship records.
- Original rapport, Outreach Quality and Outbound Department owners review exact draft evidence, copy, formatting and sales-stage appropriateness.
- Sales Director owns authentic reply interpretation, qualification and handoff.
- Original Outbound Governor and approved sending provider enforce exact recipient identity, source, message, current history, original founder approval and send receipts. All unknowns must **fail closed**.
- Existing commercial email audit measures genuine delivery failures, authentic replies, conversations, qualified opportunities, accepted proposals, paying customers and cleared revenue by industry and approach.

Do not create new agent owners, contact databases, competing sender transports, campaigns or schedulers under this directive. Preserve previously approved and sent messages.

### Release and measurement rules

No unauthorised email send, publication, spending, purchase, campaign activation, database migration, security relaxation or restart. Require original founder approval wherever policy already requires it. The last recipient-bound sending step must independently revalidate actual source, original contact history and canonical suppression.

**This policy document is normative; it does not prove universal software enforcement.** EmpireOS draft checks cannot intercept all external Gmail/manual send actions or independently authenticate caller-supplied facts. Verify each real writer and sender route separately; hold unverified routes.

Report **Completed | Evidence | Outstanding | Next action**. Distinguish proposed, implemented, tested, authorised, released, delivered, paid and revenue recognised.
