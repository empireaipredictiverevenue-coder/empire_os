# EmpireOS — Free & paid sources of qualified US roofing-call traffic
Date: 27 October 2026 | Commercial acquisition intelligence | OBSERVE / no spend

## Answer to the founder
The business has *two* marketplaces: find homeowners who need roofing services, and find buyers authorised to receive and pay for those enquiries. Treat publisher relationships and traffic acquisition separately from buyer recruiting; never count a company outreach reply as consumer traffic.

**A cost-free media placement is not a zero-cost business activity**: it still requires labour, accurate creative, permission and time. A CPL/rev-share partnership is performance-*paid*, not free just because no CPC is bought upfront.

### No-media-spend discovery: priority sequence
1. **Organic search:** read the *existing* GSC query/page evidence to find actual homeowner interest and fix genuinely useful content with the original SEO/site owners; no programmatic doorway-style ZIP duplication.
2. **Earned local publisher referrals:** identify useful homeowner publications, community newsletters, home-improvement resources and third-party articles; check their audiences, source terms and genuine editorial permission. Do not spam webmasters, buy fake backlinks or auto-post.
3. **YouTube videos and Shorts:** accurate, non-robotic, useful contractor-selection, roof-damage education and cost explainer content; measure referral visits. Video views aren't site leads.
4. **Helpful community responses:** relevant questions in permitted forums / Reddit / Facebook groups; no automated account farming, repeated self-links, unsolicited DMs, misleading claims or hidden affiliation.
5. **Prior opt-in audience:** newsletter/word of mouth only after source consent, suppression and law checks. No homeowner audience presently established.
6. **Nextdoor local free pages:** only if a real eligible local service provider is accurately represented. Empire as a remote lead publisher does not automatically qualify.
7. **Pinterest organic:** a later mid-funnel content test if genuine advice/media exists.

### Paid research (not activated)
1. **Google Search:** responsive search ads + call assets (legacy call-only sunset February 2027); geo/intent targeting and source policy review required. Test only with real per-call settlement and cost tracking.
2. **Microsoft/Bing Search:** call extensions/reporting, similar geo/intent constraints. Compare actual CPC versus accepted calls after approved trial.
3. **Meta Facebook/Instagram call ads:** direct calls or web forms; quality, consent and funnel track separately.
4. **Nextdoor local ads:** local advertiser/provider eligibility and accurate brand representation required.
5. **Approved publisher CPL/pay-per-call partnerships:** negotiate definitions, exclusivity, allowed sources, caller consent and reversals. Paying a publisher from commissions is still a cost.
6. **Pinterest paid:** consideration/leads CPC/CPA possibilities; usually less immediate than high-intent roofing searches.
7. **Reddit paid lead ads:** web lead-conversion objective; does not guarantee direct-call features or viable CPA.
8. **Google Local Services Ads:** exclude from Empire resale/rerouting: Google explicitly prohibits lead selling and forwarding to unrelated businesses.

- **Effective cost per payable call:** actual channel spend / independently accepted payable calls (undefined until both denominators available).
- **Net actual margin:** money independently paid minus media, publisher, processing, corrections and refunds.
- **No profit or ROI claims:** no verified traffic-source-level call sample, CPC purchases, source acceptance, paid campaigns or settled test margin yet. All numeric conversion assumptions stay NULL.
- **Paid test authority:** required separately: buyer approved media source, current geography/hours and payout terms; ad platform policy; responsible advertiser identity; funded cap; attribution and call recording/legal notice; bounded stop; independent final settlement validation.
