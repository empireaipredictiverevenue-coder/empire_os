## No-access predictive pipeline scorecard

| Stage | Evidence worth measuring | Decision supported |
|---|---|---|
| Outbound prospect → accepted discovery | Unique dated accepted meeting, account and reason | Which messages lead to useful discovery? |
| Discovery → qualified technical evaluation | Buyer problem, agreed integration, owner and timeline | Where should solutions engineers invest time? |
| Evaluation → contracted booking | Signed contract and recognised booking amount; segment/date | Which cohorts yield a dependable forecast? |
| Signed booking → active first integration | Confirmed activation and time-to-first-value, not only a signed deal | Is the handoff delivering useful customer capability? |
| Activation → retained or expanded revenue | Real renewal, paid expansion and verified risk event | Which early signals predict durable revenue? |

**Illustration only:** an announced 3 meetings/week goal for a 13-week quarter implies **39 target meetings**, NOT 39 actually held meetings, opportunities or bookings. No revenue forecast is defensible without verified conversion, ACV and recognition evidence.
