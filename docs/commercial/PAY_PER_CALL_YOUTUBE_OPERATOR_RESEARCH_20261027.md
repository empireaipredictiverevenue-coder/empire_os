# EmpireOS Pay-Per-Call YouTube Operator Research
**Research date:** 27 October 2026 | **Mode:** public-source review / OBSERVE | **Scope:** US roofing and transferable call operations

## Evidence limitations
This is a **real research pass of official published video pages, one full operator-hosted transcript and published case studies**, not a claim that seven complete YouTube videos were played and transcribed. Video titles or descriptions alone do not verify the strategies taught inside them. Vendor case results are self-reported and are **not** independently audited profit or transferable results for Empire AI.

## Additional grounded operator and partner lessons
- **WeCall Media / Ringba case study (2020):** https://www.ringba.com/blog/wecall-media-case-study. The operator describes learning that cheap callers weren't always useful, reviewing routing/no-answer and buyer acceptance, and prioritising publisher quality. A historical reported revenue milestone is vendor marketing, not proof of profit.
- **Perform[cb] Medicare channel case:** https://www.performcb.com/case-studies/study/medicare-marketer-boosts-call-volume-with-new-traffic-channels/. Search/social/display diversification and verified caller qualification matters; health-insurance results are not roofing benchmarks. Past call-only ad formats must be rechecked against current platform rules.
- **UNiK360 intermediary thesis:** https://unik360.beehiiv.com/p/march-21-2024. A claimed no-ads call-broker strategy is joining publishers with buyers on commercial terms; eight-figure marketing figures have not been independently audited. No call reselling, direct outreach or new brokerage activation is authorised here.

## What EmpireOS should actually learn
1. **Traffic:** First-party GSC / measured publisher referrals / source-labeled real homeowner sessions. Search and YouTube media creative is owned by existing specialist teams. Paid Google/Microsoft/Meta is a future funded, separately authorised trial—not an execution instruction.
2. **Conversion/CRO:** Differentiate mere CTA clicks, actual ZIP coverage checks, consented call choice, connected calls and qualified acceptance; cohort-based funnel statistics only.
3. **Call-quality diagnostics:** The original commercial owner can report aggregated, permitted reasons: no answer, busy, wrong geography, service mismatch, short call, no applicable buyer, declined/invalid source, refunded. Keep personal identifiers and recordings within authorised providers; never expose protected calls to competitor research.
4. **Publisher/buyer management:** Maintain evidence of published source terms, approved publisher IDs, buyer-specific traffic permission, opening hours and capacity; do not silently place one caller with multiple buyers.
5. **Revenue/CRU candidate:** Count verified accepted/settled outcomes and actual costs, not hypothetical payouts or old operator revenue case claims; do not infer profitable keywords from YouTube success stories.
