## Evidence contract — exactly six stages
1. **First-party arrival:** timestamped source/referrer and landing session from privacy-appropriate first-party measurement. Do not substitute GSC impressions; no spoofable UTM claims of causal proof.
2. **Eligibility:** real user-initiated ZIP/service inquiry checked against current buyer coverage and operating hours; no address/person harvest for this research report.
3. **Consumer decision:** clear call choice, consent and recording notice; actual site handoff. A CTA click alone is not a connected call.
4. **Call provider:** real provider call ID and campaign; actual connected duration, route and source. No fake calls or simulated revenue.
5. **Buyer disposition:** independent billable acceptance subject to written geography, service type, duration, duplicate and rejection conditions. Advertised payout prior isn't earned revenue.
6. **Settlement:** original finance owner reconciles remittance and reversals. Only then compute attributable net margins.

## Measurement definitions
- Source to eligible-interaction rate: eligible user interactions divided by valid first-party landing sessions.
- Eligible interaction to call-choice rate: consented call choices divided by eligible interactions.
- Call-choice to connected rate: real connected calls divided by choices.
- Connected to accepted rate: buyer-accepted payable calls divided by real connected calls.
- Cost per payable call: actual attributable source expenditure divided by accepted payable calls.
- Net settled margin: independently settled money minus real source and processing costs and reversals.
- A missing numerator, denominator, source link or finance receipt remains **UNKNOWN**, never zero or a forecast.
