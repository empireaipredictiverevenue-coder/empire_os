## A useful five-stage operating process

| Stage | Entry evidence | Exit rule | Owner and next action |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Accepted lead | Unique company identity, attributed source and legitimate enquiry | Sales accepts; category and business problem understood | One named owner; meaningful next step and time |
| 2. Discovery/demo | A genuine accepted meeting | Demo held and actual outcome/stakeholders recorded | Owner logs dated follow-up |
| 3. Trial/POC | Buyer-approved success criteria, sponsor and agreed period | Accepted, rejected or closed with recorded outcome | Owner checks criteria and next step date |
| 4. Commercial decision | Buyer-ready proposal and decision timeline | Signed scope or declined proposal with reason | Commercial owner, scoped offer and authorised price |
| 5. Won/activated or lost | Independent signed terms OR documented loss | Genuine onboarding/activation; settlement verified separately | Customer-success owner and independent payment record |

A proposal, contract, activated account and cash realised are distinct events. Never count a trial as a paying customer.

## Minimum fields for a four-person team

At account level: attributed source, unique company key, buyer need and segment. At deal level: account_key, stage, responsible owner, latest meaningful buyer event date, dated next action, buyer-approved trial success criteria, buyer budget if supplied, seller estimate distinct from contract amount, loss reason, contract evidence reference. Do not require raw call audio, customer credentials or personal data for the first overview.

## Weekly founder scorecard — no invented numbers

| Question | Evidence rule | Initial status |
|---|---|---|
| Which deal needs attention? | Next action date passed and still active | **Unknown** — no authorised client records |
| What is stuck? | Days since last buyer-confirmed progression | **Unknown** |
| Do demos lead to trials? | Unique genuine trial starts / completed eligible demos in same cohort | **Unknown** |
| Which trials convert? | Distinct signed deals / completed trials within agreed lag window | **Unknown** |
| Why did we lose? | Documented mutually exclusive buyer loss reasons | **Unknown** |
| Is forecasting honest? | Show estimated pipeline, bookings, activation and cash separately | **Unknown** |
| Are clients activated? | Signed contracts with proved activation and accountable owner | **Unknown** |

## Ten-business-day conditional pilot

Days 1–2: agree stage dictionary, field ownership and scope using 5–10 client-authorised anonymised records. Days 3–4: test account deduplication, next steps, demo and trial evidence against read-only records. Days 5–6: produce source-stage tables and a list of real exceptions, distinguishing estimated pipeline from signed bookings and settled cash. Days 7–8: prepare two controlled automation proposals (missing next step, stale trial); use synthetic data **only in isolated nonproduction tests**. Days 9–10: deliver one-page management scorecard, evidence glossary, implementation backlog, acceptance checks and rollback plan.

**No customer CRM integration, call recording, automated follow-up, data migration or outcome is claimed or performed.** Real implementation requires appropriately skilled delivery ownership and contractual authority.
