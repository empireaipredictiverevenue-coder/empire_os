## Commercially relevant findings, appropriately limited

UK: Building regulation approval and planning permission are different requirements. Replacement roof coverings may need building control approval or qualifying self-certification. Some ordinary repairs can be exempt, and roof work that does not materially change external appearance often does not need planning permission; local restrictions and individual project facts still govern. GOV.UK lists roofing-related competent person schemes, but operator inclusion does not prove a particular roofer's current credentials.

USA: The City of Chicago publishes a building-permits dataset covering issued permits from 2006 onward, omitting subsequently voided or revoked permits. The original official metadata exposes 122 declared columns including fields that can be sensitive or property-specific. We fetched **zero permit records** and made **no assumption** that an issued permit means a new homeowner lead, open procurement, approved contractor or payable call. Chicago's data portal refers licensing to its Terms of Use, which have not been accepted or approved for resale or targeting.

These sources could eventually support a rights-cleared **aggregate market intelligence** product, independently cited building-control guidance, or permitted planning/permit demand-gap research. They cannot justify unconsented household targeting, automatic buyer matching, direct outreach, source data resale, commercial revenue forecasting or consumer claims.
