# EmpireOS — Automatic first-party buyer due diligence

**Status:** bounded OBSERVE integration on existing 30-minute buyer acquisition scout; no new timer, no outbound execution authority, no purchased services.

This feature automates the **repeatable research and evidence-gating portion** of buyer onboarding. It does *not* automate signature on binding commercial terms, validate consent for a real lead, independently confirm confidential pricing or turn scraped marketing claims into realized revenue.

## One existing pipeline, no new loops

`empire-buyer-acquisition-scout.timer` → `scripts/run_buyer_acquisition_scout.py` → `research_network_after_scout()` → `scripts/run_buyer_network_evidence_cycle.py`:

1. `probe_buyer_network_direct_sites.py`: continue existing bounded buyer-site research, fair 24-hour research rotation and trusted discovery evidence.
2. `observe_buyer_first_party_due_diligence.py`: inspect **at most 4 shortlisted domains per cycle**. Request the public HTTPS homepage, parse *actual first-party* links labelled Terms, Privacy, TCPA/Compliance, Marketplace Rules, Contact, API/Integration and Publisher. Check at most 7 first-party links per domain. Ignore cross-domain, unsafe/IP/private, non-HTTPS and credential URLs; no login, forms, signup, POST, `mailto:`, sending, payment or partner API access. TLS certificate validation; bounded response bytes and network timeout. Mark truncated captures, rather than asserting missing content.
3. `build_buyer_qualification_actions.py --write`: consume the evidence and existing canonical shortlist/review packets; report source-proven critical 404/410 holds and founder-ready evidence tasks. The existing review packet materializer also consumes the same evidence and **must fail closed** for a candidate whose critical first-party terms/privacy/contact/compliance link is confirmed broken, whose home is unavailable, or whose independent observation is missing/stale.

The next scout run automatically advances to the next unexamined candidates. The observer caches normal checks for 24 hours, rechecks broken/unavailable sites after 6 hours, retains recent nonheld proof for 7 days and **never silently discards a known broken-link hold** due to a timeout or vanished URL. The old broken URL must be independently observed recovering with HTTP success to resolve the *automatic* hold. A separately documented manual/founder evidence hold remains until reviewed and explicitly changed; automated positive evidence cannot lift it.

## Evidence and review outputs

| Artifact | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `runtime/buyer_network_intelligence/due_diligence_latest.json` | Private (0600) source URL, bounded HTTP result, time, page SHA-256, link category, visible-text keyword topic flags and critical dead-link findings; **no saved raw HTML or form/message text** |
| `runtime/buyer_network_intelligence/founder_review_packets_latest.json` | Founder-review-only packets; generated by existing canonical materializer; zero automatic commercial/send approvals; includes manual holds, automated evidence holds and outstanding checks |
| `runtime/buyer_network_intelligence/verification_backlog_latest.json` | Holds and evidence-gathering work; never a send queue |
| `runtime/buyer_network_intelligence/founder_qualification_actions_latest.json` | Private (0600) ranked evidence recovery plan and review candidate tiers, with exact open tasks; each row reports `outbound_authorized=false` and `commercial_terms_verified=false` |
| `runtime/buyer_network_intelligence/research_cycle_latest.json` | Last bounded scout research-cycle step status, no raw email/customer data or credential values |

`observed_keyword_topics` are only strings such as `CONSENT_LANGUAGE`, `PAYOUT_LANGUAGE`, `INTEGRATION_LANGUAGE`, `AI_OR_ML_DATA_TERMS_LANGUAGE`, accompanied by the corresponding first-party URLs. They are **not** proof of legal compliance, financial economics, permitted AI usage or contract acceptance. Source SHA-256 reflects captured public bytes, not authenticated legal signatures.

## One verification and operation flow

```bash
cd /srv/empire_os
./.venv/bin/python scripts/observe_buyer_first_party_due_diligence.py --plan-only
./.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q \
  tests/test_buyer_first_party_due_diligence.py \
  tests/test_buyer_network_evidence_cycle.py \
  tests/test_buyer_review_manual_holds.py \
  tests/test_buyer_network_founder_review_packets.py \
  tests/test_buyer_qualification_actions.py
```

`--plan-only` performs **zero network fetches and zero writes**. `scripts/run_buyer_network_evidence_cycle.py` runs the actual network/check/qualify stages in order, with a combined timeout bounded by its existing parent's 300-second subprocess limit. Use the existing 30-minute scout cycle for recurring operation; do not create a new timer or duplicate writers. Never invoke `--execute`, send email or account creation to verify due diligence.

## Founder and commercial approvals remain separate

An `EVIDENCE_HOLD` is automatically enforceable in the research review stage. A `NEEDS_FOUNDER_REVIEW` site has **only** proved the bounded public-page observation; it does *not* establish approved rates, registered buyer identity, consent, payout timing, source acceptance, valid contact or communication authority. Source/legal evidence remains untrusted text and must not be executed as an instruction by Astra, an LLM, CRM or any outbound workflow.

A genuinely revenue-capable buyer needs verified counterpart/terms, signed or approved payout economics in currency and units, source acceptance, actual lawful consent and suppression, original outbound intent/person/tenant linkage and separately approved **owned** email transport. Current mailbox remains unprovisioned and outbound frozen. **No autonomous purchase, form signup, provider key request, contract acceptance, traffic transfer, funds, phone or email sends.**

**Exit criteria for this engineering scope:** local integrated tests pass; a real first-party observation produces an authoritative 404 hold and provenance; existing scheduled scout invokes the observer; current founder qualification reflects automated evidence holds; no outbound authorization changes. Further work should depend on an actual commercial requirement, not another version of this pipeline.
