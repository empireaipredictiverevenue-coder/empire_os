## Evidence-first blueprint: 12–24-month enterprise deal cycles

A technically defensible pilot would begin by mapping the buyer's *actual* sales process to immutable stage-exit proof rather than configuring arbitrary automation first.

| Sales transition | Required evidence (example, not the buyer's real data) | Reject / hold condition |
|---|---|---|
| Lead → Qualified evaluation | Named sales owner; buyer's stated use case; segment; first-party source and permitted contact context | A website hit or SDR opinion alone does not verify enterprise buying intent |
| Evaluation → Technical fit | Buyer-approved success criteria, sponsor, assessed deployment requirements, next decision date | A demo booking without completed assessment does not qualify |
| Technical fit → Commercial validation | Recorded champion, decision process, economic buyer or evidence still pending, security/procurement gaps with owners | MEDDPICC labels without underlying buyer-supplied evidence cannot close a gate |
| Commercial validation → Contract approved | Authorised agreement, legal entity, price/currency, term, scope and effective date | A seller's projected contract or unsigned order is not booked business |
| Approved contract → Realised economic outcome | Approved fulfilment record and independently reconciled bank settlement / accounting evidence | A won-stage timestamp, invoice or forecast is not settled revenue |

Every change records source, timestamp, who assessed it, policy version, and explicit unknowns. Allow qualified exceptions via approved reviewer audit trail, not silent bypass.
