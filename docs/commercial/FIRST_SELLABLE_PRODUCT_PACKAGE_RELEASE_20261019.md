## Available safe operating paths

A. Seller can create/log in to Gumroad and upload the customer ZIP as
a draft unlisted digital product. Verify identity/payout, copyright,
refund/support, product images and terms; run a **test purchase**
(not a self-purchase with a real card) then approve listing + price.
Do not count an uploaded draft as published or a registered user as
a buyer.

B. For the already historically registered Apify account:
open the console, authenticate, create a new Python Actor by ZIP
source upload, inspect Docker build and *free/test-only* sample,
review platform resources, complete billing/KYC, configure a
per-event pricing plan only after approved commercial calculation,
then publish after owner review.

## Required founder decisions before public charging

1. Authorise final product title/brand, one-time price and currency.
2. Choose the first seller platform/account and sign in there.
3. Complete required individual/sole-trader seller identity and
   legal/payment onboarding (do not paste credentials in ChatGPT).
4. Confirm applicable refund/licence, buyer support contact,
   privacy/copyright, purchase confirmation and fulfilment.
5. Approve public listing / first charge separately.
6. Verify published URL, test checkout, transaction receipts,
   delivery hash, refunds and first collected payout.
7. Do not cross-sell unverified raw lead inventory to any account.
