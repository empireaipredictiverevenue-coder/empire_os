## Verified economics instead of a pretend commission

For each **actually completed and paid installation**:

- Money collected = actual settled payout from the installer programme, not its public advertisement.
- Net distributable amount = maximum of zero and (cash collected less refunds/reversals, handling cost and agreed reserve).
- Solar Briton payout = separately negotiated share of net distributable amount, or a fixed fee that is demonstrably supported by the same actual completed and paid event. Not a fixed rate for an unpaid enquiry without a different legitimate paying buyer.
- Empire margin = net distributable amount minus paid publisher share, subject to real delivery/admin costs.

With HPO's published **£300 gross** headline, £10,000 in gross programme earnings would require **at least 34 successfully completed, qualified installations**, *before* publisher payout and other expenses. This is illustrative arithmetic, not an earned or expected amount. At its published 28-working-day-after-completion payout terms, this is not the fastest credible first-cash lane.
