## What a single enterprise buyer is actually buying

Deliverables must specify:

- **Entity and geographic scope:** a named region, trade/sector and
  evidence-backed source identity, not unbounded scraped contact lists.
- **Evidence type and freshness:** raw source observation versus
  independent date of underlying event, current ranking/click metrics
  versus historical archive and any known lag, error rate or missingness.
- **Actionable decisions:** what changed, why a commercial buyer should
  review it, confidence level and who is accountable for verification.
- **Rights and usage:** original report versus derived analysis, API
  licence and any redistribution/exclusivity limits; OGL attribution
  only for fields actually licensed and reviewed.
- **Commercial terms:** service levels, permitted users and territories,
  cancellation/corrections, delivery format, data retention and
  auditability. No unsupported model accuracy or guaranteed lead counts.

## Data rights boundary is a commercial asset, not an obstacle to bypass

The UK Open Government Licence v3 permits commercial exploitation of
**information actually covered by the licence**, subject to attribution
and exclusions including personal data and third-party rights.
Existing V18/V19 Empire rights reviews still have eight independent
release controls open; V20 permits a narrow eight-field source
projection but does not sign off every transient raw response.

The UK's ICO explains that selling or renting details for direct
marketing is data sharing and requires lawful, transparent collection,
applicable marketing permissions, rights management and opt-outs.
Do not resell customer contacts, homeowner leads or consumer profiles
through this portfolio.

Original analyses may be offered under their own terms once exact
buyer requirements, source reuse scope and delivery authority are
verified. AI assistance and provenance should be disclosed as
appropriate; hashes are not external IP certifications.

## Revenue-first execution order

**Stage A — Buyer discovery now:** propose the $199 SPC scope only
in response to an authentic decision-maker's expressed interest.
Separately approach relevant agency/consultancy partners when identity,
suppression and message approval are satisfied. Ask which recurring
commercial decisions they already pay analysts to solve. Log actual
answers in the existing CRM, not fabricated interviews.

**Stage B — Small recurring pilot:** offer one agency a clearly bounded
$1,250/month white-label *original analysis* pilot conditional on
genuine current observations, accepted scope and fulfilment capacity.
If a different price is accepted, record the actual terms rather
than rewriting source evidence to support a hypothesis.

**Stage C — High value:** after one or two paying pilots with evidence,
prepare a $4,000/month multi-market territory assessment or bespoke
$7,500 diligence project for a qualifying enterprise buyer.
Do not issue exclusive rights on public/open source underlying data
that Empire does not exclusively own.

**Stage D — API and marketplaces:** only when monthly signal updates,
entity deduplication, accuracy/latency bounds, chargeable delivery
rights and real contracts have been proven, consider the existing
EmpireOS API + AWS/Snowflake/Databricks distribution options.

**Independent payment, settlement and fulfilled report evidence are
required** before any amount enters realised revenue or Economic Memory.
No current forecast should count proposal values as cash.

## Implemented in canonical EmpireOS after scoped promotion

`empire_os/data_brokerage_monetization_portfolio.py` is a pure
offer/channel portfolio builder; it references the four source product
IDs from the existing Data Handler and rejects any forged source rights,
customer billing, outbound or publication state. It records all prices
as hypotheses, and marks every account, offer, entitlement, payment
and production API publication **OFF**.

`scripts.preview_data_brokerage_monetization --read-only` reads the
existing private 0600 internal catalog, checks its source trust gates
and prints a concise portfolio count. `--show-options` prints the
internal commercial options without raw third-party records or buyer PII.
It performs no writes, API network requests or external marketplace
signups; it does not touch the flagship website.

No new tenant, database, service, source capture, cloud compute,
payments, billable tools, outbound schedule or customer portal was
introduced. Keep all other agent work isolated.
