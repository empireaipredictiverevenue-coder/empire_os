## One measurement worth keeping separate as the team scales

Credal is working in a setting where a technical enterprise sale and an actual governed deployment involve different stakeholders, milestones and proof. The useful independent diagnostic question is: **Do first conversations, qualified security/technical evaluations and operational agent deployments appear as three distinct evidence events?**

| Stage | Possible evidence definition | What cannot be inferred |
|---|---|---|
| 1. First-touch business conversation | Unique company, accepted meeting, buyer role, discussed problem, dated outcome and channel | A cold email, marketing touch or BDR activity alone is not a qualified pipeline event |
| 2. Qualified, approved enterprise evaluation | Customer-confirmed use case, security/governance reviewer involvement, approved technical owner and a next step | Evaluation entry is not approval for production use or a signed deal |
| 3. Accepted commercial commitment | Buyer-approved scope and signed, authoritative contract/booking record | Forecast value or a sales-team closed-won click is not independently received revenue |
| 4. Operational deployment and activation | Customer-approved production go-live evidence, access controls, first productive business result and date | An integration demo does not prove a live customer's realised business benefit |
| 5. Retained or expanded revenue | Finance-issued paid/recognised amount, actual renewal/expansion and verified entity linkage | Multiple deployments are not necessarily multiple paying customers |
