## Highest-value additional Jev responsibilities — same existing stack

1. **Demand recognition / intent qualification.** Score *source-grounded*
   evidence of purchase, urgency, role and affordability, separating
   procurement from hiring, vendor pitches, public events and referrals.
   Never turn public permits or regulatory records into invented leads.
2. **Company and person research escalation.** Request separate original
   first-party business-identity checks for the most promising intent
   observations; do not assert first-party verification from social text.
3. **Buyer inventory and capacity alignment.** Once Buyer Scout confirms a
   legally sourced, actual buyer with current verified intake, match niche,
   geography, quantity, exclusions, payout and available capacity. Until
   those separate receipts exist, results must remain `CAPACITY_UNKNOWN`.
4. **Commercial next-best-review action.** Flag a buyer's question, requested
   proof, rate/terms gap or evidence needed before a proposal. Consult
   existing CRM threads, suppression and previous outreach before suggesting
   follow-up. Do not send or submit applications.
5. **Learning with economic receipts.** Use independently verified accepted
   leads/calls, paid invoices, disputes and real costs for later calibrations,
   rather than completion counters or hypothetical revenue forecasts.
   SkillForge must require owner digest approval for any reusable skill.

The portfolio implemented here is the deterministic/optional shadow reviewer
for step 1 and review routing toward step 2. Steps 3–5 require separately
grounded buyer, CRM and economic-memory connectors and their established
owners. No new orchestrator, duplicate Research Jev reflex or independent
lead database is warranted.

## Security, reliability and next gates

- Every selected actor decision is produced by `run_intent_brain_actor`,
  including its existing `adjudicate_intent` contract.
- Model sees **only** allowed niche/channel/actor type/decision/gate booleans.
  It never sees a name, social handle, URL, email or original post.
- Batch input is validated *before* any Laya calls. Maximum 60 signals
  per review and 3 explicit, local model calls. Rejected/watch signals
  cannot consume model inference. Outages hold without mutation.
- The portfolio deduplicates identical source links. It does not attribute
  resolved canonical identity, buy-side commitments, qualified lead supply,
  consent, verified buyers or payments from any signal.
- Existing **Research owner's Jev publisher reflex**, optional hosted Jev
  adapter and circuit-breaker remain separate owner-owned implementations.
  Future shared model load limiting should reuse the existing circuit breaker.
- Before internal Founder Console wiring: owner review the exact branch,
  test an authenticated session and tenant authority, audit existing CRM
  suppression/read-only controls, benchmark real labelled holdouts and verify
  cross-branch merge conflicts. No background task, outbound, CRM writes,
  funding or production deployment by this feature branch.
