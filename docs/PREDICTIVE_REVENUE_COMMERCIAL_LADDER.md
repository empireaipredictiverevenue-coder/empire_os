# Predictive Revenue Commercial Product Ladder

Status: **FOUNDER-APPROVED PRODUCT / PRICE ARCHITECTURE — 2026-09-24**

## Positioning

Predictive Revenue is Empire AI's flagship commercial engine.

The commercial hierarchy is:

1. **Self-Serve Intelligence** — low-friction evidence products.
2. **Commercial Exchange** — monthly buyer seats + usage/overage.
3. **Predictive Revenue OS** — enterprise deployments from $25k to $250k+.

Predictive Cloud remains the wider cross-system intelligence architecture.
Predictive Revenue is the commercial/revenue engine within it.

## Product principle

The enterprise buyer is not paying for a feature bundle. They are paying for a
governed system that connects real commercial evidence and answers:

- what revenue is likely;
- from whom / where / when;
- what is at risk;
- where revenue is leaking;
- what opportunity is emerging;
- what action has the strongest evidence;
- what actually became revenue / gross profit;
- what the prediction error teaches the system.

## Commercial truth

- Forecast != observed truth.
- Recommendation != execution authority.
- Proposal != contract.
- Payment request != revenue.
- Payment evidence != recognized revenue.
- Recognized revenue != realized gross profit.
- Enterprise recurring service pricing remains custom until separately approved.
- Deployment scope and economics must be verified before binding terms.
- Canonical settlement remains USDT on BSC.
- Autonomous consequential execution remains governed.
