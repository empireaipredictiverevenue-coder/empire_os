# Empire AI — Master Business & System Architecture

Date: 2026-09-22
Status: CANONICAL MASTER REFERENCE

This document is the top-level architecture reference for the Empire AI business.

Canonical organization architecture: `docs/DEPARTMENT_OPERATING_ARCHITECTURE.md`

Canonical intelligence-provider architecture: `docs/INTELLIGENCE_ROUTER_ARCHITECTURE.md`
It exists to prevent architecture drift, disconnected subsystem builds and repeated
rediscovery of the same operating model.

Canonical hierarchy:

**Empire AI → EmpireOS → Predictive Cloud → Predictive Revenue + the wider intelligence, opportunity, product and autonomous operating systems.**

Predictive Cloud is the umbrella intelligence architecture.
Predictive Revenue is the commercial engine and revenue-truth loop.
EmpireOS is the autonomous operating system that runs both.

## 15. Authority Model

### Automatic internal authority
Safe, reversible, observable internal work should not require repeated approval.

### Governed external authority
Approved external activity should operate under standing policies, caps,
suppression, compliance, idempotency and audit controls rather than repeated
manual approval for every small action.

### Founder gates
Stop only for genuinely consequential actions such as:
- material authority expansion
- destructive/irreversible infrastructure
- actual fund movement outside approved bounded rails
- legally binding founder acceptance where required
- irreversible accounting / revenue-recognition decisions
- unreviewed high-risk commercial commitments

Goal: **few meaningful gates, not constant micromanagement.**

---

## 16. Architecture Rule

Do not build new disconnected subsystems when an existing Empire capability can
be extended or composed.

Every major subsystem must be classified as:

- CONNECTED
- NEEDS WIRING
- LEGACY
- RETIRED

New engineering should prioritize **wiring the autonomous business loop** over
creating parallel replacements.

The finish line is not "more modules."

The finish line is a self-operating Empire that continuously discovers,
evaluates and pursues evidence-backed opportunities, converts them into genuine
commercial outcomes, learns from the results and reallocates itself toward
better opportunities.
