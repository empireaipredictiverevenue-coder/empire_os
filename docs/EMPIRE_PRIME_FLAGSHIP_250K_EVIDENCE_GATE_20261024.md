## What the £250,000 figure is — and is not

The founder's **£250k is a target flagship product/build valuation**, not an observed accepted market purchase price, independently appraised software asset value, a realised sale or proof of economic return. Different valuation approaches give different figures:
- **Replacement cost**: time and expense a competent team would incur to rebuild the demonstrably working, owned, secure, documented and deployable software from scratch, after excluding unrelated, duplicated or unowned code and recurring costs.
- **Product commercial value**: supported use cases, reliability, deployment rights, a measurable advantage, prospective customer demand and willingness to pay for the entire service.
- **Business valuation**: contracts, recurring *realised* revenue, margins, retention, operating risk, IP ownership, customer concentration and defensibility. It is not equal to a folder of code or to an engineering replacement-cost estimate.

Do not portray a proposed £250k cost/price/valuation as independently validated. There is no authenticated £250k offer, independent appraisal or commercial payment in this work.

## Concrete end-to-end acceptance criteria before pricing claims

1. **Reliable local reasoning:** independent owner-approved synthetic multi-turn runs measure response completion, p50/p95 end-to-end latency, resource contention, timeout rate and forced-stop behavior. A timed-out run cannot be retrospectively counted as a pass.
2. **Real authorised spoken action:** demonstrate an opted-in fictional/test-user timer or reminder with authenticated voice input, grounded intent, original permission, actual task scheduling, cancellation, delivered alert and spoken confirmation. Preserve original timezone, repeat suppression and privacy controls.
3. **Canonical source/tenant authority:** original EmpireDB and original security owners verify the identity/source/tenant binding, signed custody, replay protection, source rights, data retention, revocation, rollback and cross-tenant isolation.
4. **Digital Twin truth:** held-out blind simulation tests, negative/counterfactual trajectories, causal checks, opt-out/refusal stops, original outcome journal, and independent real-world calibration. 112 synthetic branches are coverage, not a predictive accuracy score.
5. **Flagship user experience:** verified private founder login, mobile/desktop acceptance, visual design quality, real controls that do what they say, no misleading 'live' badges, accessibility and uptime targets measured under real conditions.
6. **Commercial evidence:** authentic requested demo, buyer-specific scope, accepted offer/contract, lawful fulfillment and verified payment. Commercial evidence must remain separate from system health and simulation runs.
7. **Value assessment:** only after above, prepare a defensible independent replacement-cost estimate (owned code, architecture, testing, security, UX and deployment) and market valuation based on demonstrable utility and demand. No unsupported £250k assertion.
