## Identity

**Predictive Cloud** is the umbrella intelligence layer inside EmpireOS.

It is broader than Predictive Revenue.

Canonical hierarchy:

**Empire AI**
→ **EmpireOS**
→ **Predictive Cloud**
→ Predictive Revenue
→ Market / Demand Intelligence
→ Customer / Buyer Intelligence
→ Conversion Intelligence
→ Search / Attention / Recommendation Intelligence
→ Operational / Fulfilment Intelligence
→ Physical / Spatial Intelligence
→ Risk / Resilience Intelligence
→ Capital / Portfolio Intelligence
→ Opportunity Intelligence
→ Digital Twin / Scenario Intelligence
→ Quantitative / Decision Intelligence
→ Economic Memory / Learning

Predictive Revenue remains the commercial engine and revenue truth loop.
Predictive Cloud is the wider intelligence and decision system that predicts,
explains and evaluates economically relevant business states beyond revenue alone.

## Core Question

Predictive Revenue asks:

> What revenue is likely, from whom, when, at what economics, and what became real?

Predictive Cloud also asks:

- What changed?
- Why did it change?
- What is likely to happen next?
- Where is demand forming?
- Where is risk increasing?
- Which market, customer, product, asset or channel matters most?
- Which constraint will prevent the outcome?
- What action has the strongest evidence and expected economics?
- What additional evidence is worth acquiring?
- What did the real outcome teach us?

## Canonical Intelligence Loop

OBSERVED EVIDENCE
→ ENTITY / IDENTITY RESOLUTION
→ TEMPORAL FEATURES
→ DESCRIPTIVE ANALYSIS

→ PREDICTION / FORECAST

→ VERIFIED OUTCOME

No score, forecast, recommendation or simulation silently becomes observed truth,
commercial intent, execution authority, payment or revenue.

## Data & Decision Intelligence

Data analysis is a canonical Predictive Cloud capability.

### Descriptive — what happened?
- counts, rates, distributions and cohorts;
- time series and rolling windows;
- source / market / buyer / product / channel performance;
- freshness, missingness and quality.

### Diagnostic — why?
- funnel decomposition;
- segment and cohort comparison;
- bottlenecks and anomalies;
- source / buyer / offer / geography attribution;
- correlation explicitly separated from causation.

### Predictive — what is likely next?
- commercial probabilities;
- demand / revenue / GP / traffic forecasts;
- churn / expansion / capacity risk;
- trend / regime detection;
- uncertainty and prediction intervals.

### Prescriptive — what should we do or investigate?
- next-best action;
- risk-adjusted opportunity ranking;
- capital / resource recommendations;
- experiment proposals;
- information-gathering proposals;
- recommendation only until execution authority exists.

### Causal — what actually changed the outcome?
- controlled experiments;
- holdouts;
- incrementality;
- uplift;
- treatment-vs-control;
- forecast-vs-actual.

## Quant Brain

The canonical Quantitative Intelligence architecture is the mathematical verifier
beneath Predictive Cloud.

It covers:
- robust descriptive statistics;
- Bayesian updating;
- calibration and forecast error;
- expected economics;
- downside / tail-risk;
- Monte Carlo / scenario distributions;
- causal / experiment analysis;
- constrained optimization;
- portfolio intelligence;
- value of information;
- forecast ensembles;
- later sequential decisioning when real outcome volume justifies it.

LLMs may explain and propose. The Quant layer must measure.

## Digital Twin Role

Digital Twins are state-and-scenario models inside Predictive Cloud.

Supported twin classes include:
- company / account;
- buyer;
- market / corridor / territory;
- product / offer;
- campaign / channel;
- property / physical asset;
- Empire portfolio.

A Digital Twin is not factual truth by itself. Observed facts, modeled state,
scenario state and verified outcomes remain distinguishable.

## Founder Console Surface

Founder Console should expose a Predictive Cloud command surface that answers:

1. What changed?
2. What matters?
3. What is predicted?
4. How uncertain is it?
5. What is the downside?
6. What opportunity is emerging?
7. What is blocking us?
8. What should be investigated or done next?
9. What actually happened?
10. What did the system learn?

Every answer must carry evidence status:
**OBSERVED / INFERRED / FORECAST / SCENARIO / UNKNOWN / VERIFIED OUTCOME**.

## Build Rule

Do not rebuild mature intelligence modules merely to satisfy this architecture.
Converge existing Revenue Intelligence, Conversion Intelligence, Revenue Pulse,
Market GPS, Search Intelligence, Omega/Cortex, Digital Twin, Trend/Regime,
Experiment Analysis, TimesFM shadow, physical intelligence and Founder surfaces
into this shared Predictive Cloud contract.

Predictive Cloud succeeds when Empire can move from evidence to a calibrated,
economically useful decision faster than competitors, then prove whether that
decision was right from the real outcome.
