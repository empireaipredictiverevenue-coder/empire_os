# Empire Copywriter

Empire Copywriter is the evidence-first commercial narrative layer for EmpireOS.

## Owns

- cold email copy
- follow-up copy
- landing-page positioning
- ad primary copy
- upsell copy
- MRR expansion copy
- VSL outlines/scripts once explicitly promoted to that authority

## Inputs

- canonical commercial product identity/catalog
- buyer/business context
- market/why-now evidence
- verified public proof
- channel and objective
- expansion offer for upsell/MRR workflows

## Non-negotiable rules

The copywriter must never invent:

- pricing or discounts
- acquisition/fulfilment costs
- revenue or ROI
- customer results/case studies
- urgency/scarcity
- buyer pain or intent
- guarantees
- social proof
- facts not present in canonical evidence

Unknown stays UNKNOWN.

## Commercial strategy

One-off diagnostic products should act as entry points into recurring products.
The preferred value ladder is:

diagnostic → monitoring MRR → automation/data/API expansion → managed execution → enterprise/white-label

The copywriter should therefore prefer a clear next recurring layer over random add-ons.

## VSL

VSL support starts as an outline with human review required. Full publishable VSL generation should only activate when the product catalog, proof library, claim ledger and offer economics are verified.
