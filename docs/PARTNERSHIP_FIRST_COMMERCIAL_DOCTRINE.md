# Empire AI — Partnership-First Commercial Doctrine

Date: 2026-10-01  
Status: CANONICAL COMMERCIAL DOCTRINE / FOUNDER APPROVED

## Core principle

**Relationship first. Partnership as the destination. Product and price support the conversation; they do not lead it.**

Empire AI commercial development must not operate as:

`CONTACT -> PITCH -> PRICE -> CLOSE`

The canonical commercial motion is:

`DISCOVER -> UNDERSTAND -> CONNECT -> BUILD TRUST -> SHARE INTELLIGENCE -> PROVE VALUE -> PILOT -> PARTNERSHIP -> COMMERCIALISE -> EXPAND -> LEARN`

The objective is to become commercially useful to the counterparty before asking them to buy.

## Why this exists

Empire AI is building Predictive Revenue infrastructure, not a commodity outbound machine.

The strongest long-term commercial relationships may include:

- recurring intelligence;
- opportunity or permit feeds;
- buyer capacity;
- private-capital intelligence;
- white-label products;
- API/data access;
- territory relationships;
- lead/call supply;
- revenue-share structures;
- portfolio or multi-business deployments;
- managed Predictive Revenue services.

A narrow product sale may be the first transaction, but the operating objective is a durable commercial partnership with measurable mutual value.

## Partnership-first rules

1. **Understand before pitching.**
   Research the company, person, current priorities, geography, market and likely commercial context using real evidence.

2. **Lead with relevance.**
   First contact should explain why Empire AI believes a conversation may be useful to that specific counterparty.

3. **Give before asking.**
   Where evidence permits, share a useful observation, signal, opportunity, market insight or diagnostic angle before asking for a commitment.

4. **Ask discovery questions.**
   Learn priorities, capacity, geography, sectors, deal criteria, delivery preferences, bottlenecks and success criteria.

5. **Prove value before expanding scope.**
   Use a bounded pilot, sample, mapped opportunity set or evidence-backed demonstration where appropriate.

6. **Product and price are supporting context.**
   Pricing may be stated when relevant or required for transparency, but it must not replace discovery or partnership positioning.

7. **Do not invent fit.**
   Unknown buyer need, demand, budget, authority or intent remains unknown until observed.

8. **No artificial urgency.**
   Use genuine evidence-backed timing only.

9. **No pressure after resistance.**
   Objections are discovery input. Negative intent and opt-outs are respected immediately.

10. **Suppression is absolute.**
    No partnership objective overrides DNC, opt-out, suppression or legal/compliance controls.

11. **Founder gates remain.**
    Partnership-first positioning creates no new outbound, commercial, payment, contract or revenue-recognition authority.

## Canonical relationship states

Commercial relationship state should converge toward:

`DISCOVERED`
-> `PERSON_BOUND`
-> `PARTNERSHIP_CANDIDATE`
-> `ENGAGED`
-> `NEEDS_DISCOVERED`
-> `VALUE_PROVEN`
-> `PILOT`
-> `COMMERCIAL_PARTNER`
-> `EXPANSION`

These states describe relationship maturity. They do not prove revenue.

Revenue truth still requires governed commercial evidence, real terms, payment evidence, fulfilment/outcome evidence and recognition under the canonical revenue rules.

## Message doctrine

### Good first-contact structure

1. Person-specific opening.
2. Evidence-backed reason for contact.
3. One useful observation or capability relevant to them.
4. A low-friction discovery question.
5. Optional commercial context only when useful.
6. Clear opt-out.

### Avoid

- generic “we can grow your business” claims;
- leading with a price table;
- pretending a product is a fit before discovery;
- feature dumping;
- fake scarcity;
- fabricated ROI;
- asking for a meeting before establishing relevance;
- treating every reply as a closing opportunity;
- sending a payment link before commercial fit and authority are established.

## Examples

### 1. Contractor / Permit Intelligence

**Weak product-first approach**

> We sell Permit Intelligence for $499/month. Would you like to subscribe?

**Partnership-first approach**

> Adam, I noticed CooperBuild is active in markets where permit and project-start timing can matter. We are mapping current permit activity into a filtered opportunity feed. Before I assume it is useful, which geographies and project types matter most to your team? If there is a fit, I can map the current feed against that and show you what we are actually seeing.

Commercial context such as the current catalog basis can be introduced after relevance is established or included transparently without making it the core message.

### 2. Private Capital / Roll-Up Intelligence

**Weak product-first approach**

> Our Private Capital Roll-Up product is $1,500/month.

**Partnership-first approach**

> Rick, we are building live acquisition and market intelligence around sectors showing roll-up, ownership-transition and growth signals. Rather than assume it fits Kian, I would like to understand the sectors, geographies and deal themes your team is actively prioritising. If there is overlap, we can map the intelligence against those themes and see whether there is a useful partnership.

Possible expansion:
- portfolio-company intelligence;
- sector watchlists;
- acquisition signal feeds;
- private API/data access;
- managed intelligence;
- wider Predictive Revenue deployment.

### 3. Lead Buyer / Pay-Per-Call Network

**Weak approach**

> We have roofing and HVAC calls. What is your payout?

**Partnership-first approach**

> We are building supply in roofing and home services and want to understand where your current buy-side capacity is strongest. Which geographies, service lines, qualification rules and daily caps matter most right now? If our supply matches your buy box, we can start with a bounded test and build from observed quality.

Partnership path:
`BUY BOX DISCOVERY -> SMALL TEST -> QUALITY EVIDENCE -> CAPACITY EXPANSION -> RECURRING SUPPLY PARTNERSHIP`

### 4. Enterprise Predictive Revenue

First contact should seek to understand:
- revenue leakage;
- forecasting problems;
- acquisition bottlenecks;
- market expansion;
- customer value/churn;
- sales capacity;
- operational decision latency.

Do not lead with the $250k+ deployment.

The private deployment becomes relevant after value, scope and executive fit are established.

### 5. Follow-up after no reply

Do not send:

> Just checking whether you saw my previous email.

Prefer evidence-backed continuation:

> Adam, one reason I followed up is that we have continued to see permit activity in the type of market we discussed. I do not want to assume it is relevant, so if you tell me the geography/project profile you actually want, I can tell you whether our current coverage is useful. If it is not a priority, no problem.

Follow-ups should add information, clarification or value rather than merely create pressure.

### 6. Positive reply

Do not immediately force checkout.

Move to discovery:

- What are you trying to improve?
- Which markets/sectors matter?
- What is currently difficult or slow?
- How much opportunity can the team actually process?
- What would make a pilot useful?
- What evidence would they need to continue?

Then propose the smallest credible next step.

### 7. Objection

Treat an objection as information.

Examples:

**“Too expensive.”**
Ask what outcome or delivery model would make the economics sensible. Do not immediately discount.

**“We already have a provider.”**
Ask where the incumbent works well and where gaps remain. Empire may complement rather than replace.

**“Not now.”**
Capture timing if offered. Do not manufacture urgency.

### 8. Opt-out / negative intent

Immediately suppress under canonical policy.

No future “partnership” framing, alternate sender, new campaign or agent may bypass the suppression.

## Product progression

A healthy commercial path is:

`INTELLIGENCE -> CONVERSATION -> DISCOVERY -> PROOF -> PILOT -> TERMS -> PARTNERSHIP -> EXPANSION`

Possible expansion dimensions include:
- more geography;
- more business units;
- more seats;
- higher capacity;
- API integration;
- white label;
- managed service;
- exclusive territory;
- portfolio deployment;
- recurring intelligence;
- outcome-linked commercial structures where explicitly governed.

## AI Closer / Conversation OS behavior

The AI Closer should optimize for **relationship progression and commercial truth**, not premature closing.

For each eligible conversation it should determine:

- what we currently know;
- what remains unknown;
- the counterparty's expressed priority;
- the next useful question;
- the next value we can provide;
- whether a pilot is justified;
- whether commercial terms are appropriate yet;
- whether founder approval is required;
- whether suppression or other safety gates stop the conversation.

A reply is not automatically a sales-qualified opportunity.

## Evidence requirements

All partnership progression must distinguish:

- observed company/person facts;
- observed conversation intent;
- inferred fit;
- proposed commercial structure;
- verified terms;
- verified payment;
- verified fulfilment;
- realized outcome.

Never collapse these into one “deal score.”

## Metrics

Measure more than sends and replies.

Useful observed metrics include:

- person-bound contact rate;
- meaningful reply rate;
- discovery-complete conversations;
- value-proof/pilot acceptance;
- pilot-to-commercial conversion;
- partnership expansion;
- retention;
- verified MRR/ARR/GP;
- time from first useful conversation to verified commercial outcome;
- opt-out, complaint and bounce rates.

Do not fabricate relationship or partnership scores.

## Authority

This doctrine changes commercial strategy and messaging, not authority.

It does not authorize:
- live outbound without applicable approval;
- suppression bypass;
- binding terms acceptance;
- invoices/settlement;
- fund movement;
- fulfilment;
- revenue recognition.

Existing Control Fabric, Outbound Governor, suppression, commercial evidence and founder gates remain authoritative.

## Founder principle

**Empire AI should earn the right to sell by first becoming useful.**

The desired outcome is not merely a closed sale.

The desired outcome is a growing network of customers, buyers, operators and strategic counterparties who treat Empire AI as a valuable commercial partner.
