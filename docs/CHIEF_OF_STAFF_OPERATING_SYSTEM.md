# Empire AI — Chief of Staff Operating System

Date: 2026-09-20
Status: CANONICAL FOUNDER / EXECUTIVE COORDINATION MODEL

The Chief of Staff is the founder's force multiplier and the coordination
bridge between strategy and execution.

It is not another general-purpose agent and does not replace functional leaders.

## 1. Mission

**Protect founder attention, translate strategic intent into priorities, make
ownership explicit, surface conflicts/blockers, force decision clarity, and
ensure important work closes the loop.**

## 2. Chief of Staff Responsibilities

### Strategic Translation
Founder direction
-> strategic objective
-> measurable outcome
-> workstream
-> owner
-> milestones
-> blockers
-> decision rights
-> review cadence.

### Priority Management
Maintain one company priority stack across:
- Revenue
- Product
- Engineering
- Data
- AGI/Quant
- Marketing
- Sales
- R&D
- Customer Success
- Finance
- Legal/security
- Operations

### Decision Management
Every major decision gets:
- decision ID
- owner
- context
- options
- evidence
- Quant analysis where relevant
- recommendation
- approver
- deadline
- decision
- follow-through
- outcome

### Dependency / Blocker Management
Identify:
- cross-team dependencies
- founder decisions
- production gates
- technical blockers
- commercial blockers
- missing data
- approval bottlenecks
- resourcing conflicts

### Executive Information Flow
Create:
- founder daily brief
- weekly operating review
- decision pre-reads
- launch readiness
- board/investor updates later
- risk/escalation brief
- opportunity brief

## 3. Founder Attention System

Founder attention tiers:

### Tier 0 — Interrupt
Only:
- security incident
- material revenue/payment issue
- legal/compliance emergency
- production outage
- irreversible high-risk decision

### Tier 1 — Same Day Decision
- live commercial approvals
- pricing/contract exception
- major customer issue
- critical launch blocker
- capital decision

### Tier 2 — Weekly Review
- roadmap tradeoffs
- campaigns
- product/R&D bets
- staffing
- partner strategy

### Tier 3 — Delegated
Anything within functional policy/authority.

Chief of Staff's job is to reduce founder decision load, not create more.

- yesterday's verified outcomes

- revenue/MRR/gross-profit movement
- buyer/pipeline

- marketing demand

Quarterly Planning:

## 5. Priority Framework

Rank work using:
- expected strategic impact
- expected gross profit
- urgency
- dependency unblock value
- customer impact
- confidence
- risk
- reversibility
- effort
- value of information

The Chief of Staff does not invent numeric certainty; Quant Brain supplies
numbers when evidence supports them.

## 6. Functional Leadership Model

Founder / CEO
-> Chief of Staff
-> Functional leaders / Astra-coordinated operating systems:
- Product
- Engineering
- Data/Intelligence
- AGI/Quant
- Marketing
- Sales/Revenue
- R&D
- Customer Success
- Finance/Ops
- Legal/Security

Astra may coordinate machine work.
Chief of Staff coordinates organizational priorities and founder decisions.

## 7. Chief of Staff + Astra

Astra:
- machine-level operating coordinator
- reads system state
- ranks work
- routes agents/tools
- tracks runtime blockers

Chief of Staff:
- company-level prioritization
- human ownership
- founder decisions
- cross-functional alignment
- strategic communication
- escalation

Neither automatically grants itself commercial authority.

## 8. Chief of Staff Outputs

- Daily Founder Brief
- Weekly Operating Review
- Decision Register
- Priority Stack
- Dependency Map
- Blocker Register
- Launch Readiness
- Hiring/Resource Requests
- Cross-functional RACI/DACI
- Strategic Initiative Portfolio
- Follow-up / commitment tracker

## 9. Rules

- Max 3 company priorities at one level unless explicitly justified.
- Every priority has one accountable owner.
- Every meeting has a decision/outcome purpose.
- Every decision has a follow-through owner.
- Unknown metrics remain unknown.
- Do not report synthetic work as shipped.
- Do not report forecast as actual.
- Escalate decisions, not raw noise.
- Preserve founder deep-work time.
