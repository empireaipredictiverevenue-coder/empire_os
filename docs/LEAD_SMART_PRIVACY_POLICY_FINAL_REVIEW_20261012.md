## What personal information we collect
When you visit, website security and delivery systems may process request URLs, IP address, connection times, user-agent/browser characteristics and diagnostic events. The site runs behind Cloudflare and our own EmpireOS public gateway. A no-store response does not prevent infrastructure logs. Some security/diagnostic event records may be held by Cloudflare or local journald/syslog systems.
For an optional roofing service-area preview, you provide one five-digit US property ZIP code; our first-party application uses it to check a recent buyer-supplied coverage snapshot. This lookup does not ask for your name, phone or email, does not itself make a call and does not write the submitted ZIP into a standalone application customer database. The displayed result and ZIP remain in the browser component until refreshed or cleared.
If you voluntarily request a roofing phone connection after service is activated, the call may expose your telephone number, location/ZIP supplied through the buyer's IVR, timing, connected call status, duration and any recording or transcription to Ringba, Lead Smart and downstream buyer/contractor participants. Calling is optional; mere ZIP checking does not place a call.
For business enquiries, checkout and commercial interest you may supply name, work email, business name, website/domain, industry, territory/geography, annual revenue band, requested outcome and systems, product or subscription requested, wallet/payment reference, source channel and service-capacity preferences. This information is used to reply and handle your requests. Do not put sensitive personal data in public forms.

## Why we use information
Our purposes include showing content requested by visitors, validating service-area previews, receiving self-initiated commercial enquiries, preparing any requested service proposal, detecting abuse/security incidents and complying with applicable obligations. Depending on the purpose, processing may rely on legitimate interests, steps requested before entering a contract, performance of a contract, or legal obligations under the UK GDPR. Any nonessential marketing or storage-access technology requiring consent must obtain it separately, not through this privacy notice alone.

## Roofing calls, recording and access
The Ringba Direct roofing campaign configuration has recording enabled (confirmed by Lead Smart on 11 October 2026; campaign ID CA8278ed3f26e9495b881fe3833174d007). Ringba's published recording FAQ says recordings can be kept indefinitely while the account remains open and that access to a shared recording URL is not individually tracked. The buyer's test call was ended before connection and no recording resulted; that fact does not establish how future connected calls are handled. Until a buyer-authoritative decision confirms recording disabled or confirms notice, recording responsibility, access, retention/deletion and lawful consent as applicable, the consumer call service remains disabled. If enabled, the exact pre-call notice will be displayed before the call option and the necessary in-call notices must also be provided by the responsible operator.

## Sharing and international activity
Cloudflare provides network delivery and security. Our operated hosting and business mailbox providers can process the information necessary to run the site and respond to enquiries. Ringba, Lead Smart and buyer/contractor recipients would handle data from calls after a user explicitly opts to call. Relevant infrastructure, personnel and service providers may be outside the UK. The exact controller/processor roles, cross-border transfer mechanisms and recording access must be confirmed from the contracts and systems before the call feature launches.

## Retention, rights and requests
We retain enquiry and transaction information only for the operational, contractual, dispute and legal-recordkeeping purposes for which it is needed, measured against those purposes and applicable obligations. Local rsyslog's inspected rules rotate selected logs weekly, retaining four rotations, but that does not establish the full lifespan of all journald, Cloudflare, backup or other records. Recording retention is provider-dependent and may be indefinite as described above until verified; this is not a promise that Empire-AI itself can delete it.
Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, restriction, deletion, portability or objection and withdraw consent when processing rests on consent. Contact founder@empire-ai.co.uk. UK data-protection complaints may be raised with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint. Local US consumer rights may apply based on the call destination and state. We do not represent general predictive or service-coverage estimates as legally significant automated decisions about an identified consumer.
