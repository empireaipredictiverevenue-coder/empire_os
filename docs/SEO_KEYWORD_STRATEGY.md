Date: 2026-09-20

Search strategy is a revenue strategy, not a rankings project.

## 1. Objective

Build durable organic demand around the category and product portfolio while
turning keyword/search/AI-answer evidence into first-party commercial intent.

Canonical loop:

**keyword/query -> intent -> topic/asset -> search/AI visibility -> visit/use ->
first-party signal -> product fit -> opportunity -> payment -> outcome ->
recognized revenue/gross profit.**

## 5. AI Search / GEO Strategy

Optimize for:
- clear factual definitions
- entity consistency
- primary-source evidence
- structured comparisons
- original data/research
- quotable/citable insights
- direct answers
- schema
- canonical URLs
- authoritative authorship
- machine-readable data
- high-quality internal linking

AI visibility measurement:
- observed citation
- cited URL
- query/prompt
- position/context
- competitor cited
- Empire present/absent
- provenance
- observed_at

No synthetic AI visibility score without real observations.

## 6. Programmatic SEO

Allowed when pages are:
- genuinely useful
- evidence-backed
- sufficiently differentiated
- index-worthy
- internally linked
- canonicalized
- commercially relevant

Good templates:
- territory intelligence
- market reports
- permit trends
- buyer/market opportunity
- competitor intelligence
- service demand
- corridor economics

Avoid:
- doorway pages
- spun city pages
- near-duplicate filler
- fabricated local evidence
- mass indexation with no user value
