## 1. Company Mission

Empire AI is a **Predictive Revenue Operating System**.

The company does not exist to sell isolated leads, SEO, ads, CRM, scrapers,
satellite data, seats or AI agents. Those are products and execution surfaces
inside one system.

Empire's job is to:

**observe markets -> build intelligence -> predict profitable revenue ->
create/capture demand -> connect demand to buyers/capacity -> execute GTM ->
verify payment/fulfilment/outcomes -> measure realized gross profit ->
learn -> improve the next decision.**

North-star metric:

**Predicted Gross Profit -> Realized Gross Profit -> Prediction Error -> Better Allocation**

Supporting commercial metrics:
- MRR / ARR
- recognized revenue
- gross profit / gross margin
- revenue per buyer / seat / territory / corridor / product
- opportunity-to-revenue conversion
- time-to-revenue
- buyer retention / expansion
- source/channel contribution to realized revenue
- forecast/calibration error

## 2. The Canonical 12-Step Predictive Revenue Intelligence Loop

1. **Raw World**
   Open web, search, company sites, registries, permits, public records,
   weather/storm feeds, satellite/imagery metadata, community/reddit,
   market data, buyer activity, calls, CRM, campaigns, content, payments,
   fulfilment and outcomes.

2. **Source + Provenance**
   Every fact retains source, timestamp, evidence reference, confidence,
   first/last seen, and validity state. Unknown remains unknown.

3. **Temporal Intelligence Graph**
   Canonical companies, people, employment, buying roles, markets,
   products, territories, buyers, competitors, assets, events and
   relationships with history over time.

4. **Entity Resolution**
   Resolve companies/people/domains/locations into canonical identities;
   prevent duplicate or fabricated commercial entities.

5. **Signal Fusion**
   Combine independent signals across search, permits, storms, web changes,
   hiring, reviews, technology, property, market, conversation, buyer,
   payment and outcome data.

6. **Company / Person / Market Digital Twins**
   Build evidence-backed twins of companies, buyers, territories, corridors,
   markets, campaigns and products to model current state and plausible
   future state.

7. **Causal Revenue Engine**
   Distinguish correlation from causation using experiments, holdouts,
   observed outcomes, chronology and evidence quality.

8. **Offer / Deal Value / Timing Prediction**
   Predict:
   - probability of conversion
   - expected deal value
   - expected revenue
   - expected cost
   - expected gross profit
   - time-to-revenue
   - best offer/product
   - best buyer
   - best territory/corridor
   - best channel/timing
   - churn/renewal/expansion likelihood

9. **Decision Engine**
   Astra + Predictive Cloud rank what Empire should do next based on expected
   value, confidence, risk, capacity, cost, evidence freshness and policy.

10. **Human / Agent / Channel Execution**
    Governed execution across:
    - GTM
    - content
    - SEO/AEO/GEO
    - free tools
    - social/community
    - partners/affiliates
    - outbound
    - calls/AI closer
    - campaigns
    - buyer routing
    - fulfilment
    - product delivery

11. **Payment / Fulfilment / Outcome**
    Buyer agreement, BSC USDT payment evidence, delivery, conversion,
    satisfaction, recognized revenue, costs and gross profit.

12. **Revenue Truth + Learning Loop**
    Compare prediction vs actual outcome, update calibration, improve
    opportunity ranking, buyer quality, channel allocation, offer fit,
    territory strategy and capital allocation.

No model prediction, simulation, quote or forecast is actual revenue.

### Outreach Revenue OS

Outreach is not a bulk-email subsystem. It is an evidence-backed commercial
decision layer connected to Predictive Revenue.

For every target account Empire should answer:
- Why this account?
- Why this person / buying role?
- Why now?
- Which product / offer / corridor?
- Which verified channel?
- Which proof/evidence should support the message?
- What is the expected revenue / cost / gross profit?
- What sequence is appropriate?
- What response should stop, snooze or escalate the sequence?
- Which closer/human should receive a positive reply?
- What outcome should feed the learning loop?

Core upgrades:
- trigger-based outreach from fresh market, permit, storm, territory, capacity,
  search, AI-visibility, competitor, hiring, engagement and renewal signals
- buying-committee reconstruction and multi-thread account strategy
- economic-buyer vs functional-buyer vs influencer routing
- verified person-bound channel selection
- warm-intro / partner / referral path before cold path when available
- account-based sequences rather than independent email blasts
- real proof packs and corridor/territory economics
- content-assisted outreach using reports, calculators, free tools and demos
- meeting-prep briefs and objection intelligence
- deterministic reply classification + next-action review
- deliverability, bounce, complaint, suppression and opt-out health
- dormant-account reactivation
- renewal, seat-expansion, territory-expansion and cross-sell plays
- campaign/account/outcome attribution back to realized gross profit
- sequence fatigue and stop conditions
- holdout/causal measurement before claiming incrementality

Current safe slice:
`outreach_intelligence.v1` builds an OBSERVE-only operator packet from buyer,
market, corridor, channel, signal, proof and Predictive Revenue evidence.
It has zero sending, booking, CRM mutation or buyer-activation authority.

## 12. Buyer / Seat / Territory / Corridor Commercial Model

Detailed rules live in `COMMERCIAL_BLUEPRINT.md`.

Canonical definitions:
- **Buyer**: paying commercial customer/partner
- **Territory**: geographic operating right/scope
- **Corridor**: product/niche x territory x demand type x delivery mode
- **Seat**: recurring commercial entitlement/capacity inside a corridor
- **Capacity**: verified amount buyer can accept
- **Market Lane**: broader portfolio/category grouping
- **Owned Inventory**: evidence-backed unallocated opportunity supply

Commercial principle:

**Do not blindly create supply and hope somebody buys it.**
Use observed demand, verified buyer capacity, corridor economics, expected margin,
fulfilment ability and evidence quality to decide where supply should be created.
