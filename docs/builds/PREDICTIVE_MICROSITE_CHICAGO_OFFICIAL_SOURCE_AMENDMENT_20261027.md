## Specific source-backed improvements

The pilot has been tightened to provide accurate actionable information rather than generic marketing copy:

- **Chicago Express Permit eligibility:** the City lists roof repair limited to 25% of roof area, roof recovery over one existing covering layer, and nonstructural roof replacement among eligible types. Additional layers and structural roof repairs can require architect/engineer documents. Other eligibility exclusions still apply. Source: https://chicago.gov/city/en/sites/guide-to-building-permits/home/help/faq/DOB/EPP-eligible.html
- **General permit rule:** Chicago says most construction and repair work requires a permit; owners must check their actual address, roofing scope and relevant process. Source: https://chicago.gov/city/en/sites/guide-to-building-permits/home/instructions.html
- **Permit search limits:** the City offers limited public information by property address and warns that applications older than 36 months may not appear. This is not a complete historical permit register. Source: https://webapps1.chicago.gov/buildingpermit/help
- **Contractor verification:** the City reroofing eligibility rules require a valid Illinois Roofing Contractor license for the person performing reroofing. IDFPR maintains the primary-source license lookup; this page never claims a named contractor is licensed. Source: https://idfpr.illinois.gov/checklicense.html
- **ZIP limitation:** US Census ZIP Code Tabulation Areas are geographic approximations, not USPS delivery-route boundaries or city permit-jurisdiction evidence. Source: https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/geography/guidance/geo-areas/zctas.html

These sources support editorial claims about guidance, **not** a legal determination of eligibility for any particular property, a referral offer or a prediction of market demand.
