## Founder objective: from content production to decision-grade revenue research

The previous factory was capable of creating branded research drafts and assigning `research_priority_points`, but candidates with **no official sources, no practical utility and no first-party demand** could tie at the same number as more thoroughly researched candidates. Source/brand governance was separate, correctly, but the *research investment decision* itself was insufficiently explained.

We do not need another generator, publisher, SEO crawler, AI agent, forecast database or dashboard. The existing source inputs and research queue now carry a **second, more informative executive screen**:

1. **Discovery is evidence-led:** Candidate intake remains the original `keyword_universe`, `geo_registry`, original GSC bridge and buyer/forecast owners. A keyword-score estimate, forecast or targeting suggestion is never labelled observed search demand or payable revenue.
2. **Research quality before scale:** Two distinct attributable claims and **two distinct direct official source URLs**, three specific actionable research steps, authentic geography, no location-swapped duplicate and clean basic SlopMonster pattern check. An author-entered source timestamp is subjected to a 90-day *internal desk refresh heuristic*, **not verified as true**.
3. **Original and differentiated usefulness:** Candidates must answer a real decision better than a bare government-link listing. Comparison, local context, exceptions, eligibility boundaries and a practical reader task must be evaluated by the responsible human/editorial owner; the deterministic screen does not pretend to establish originality or E-E-A-T.
4. **Acquisition truth:** Only exact-query first-party GSC signals from the original GSC source count as *observed query evidence*. They are not national keyword volume, conversion probability, page-specific traffic for an unpublished URL, or a paid acquisition target.
5. **Commercial underwriting:** A buyer ZIP/territory guidance row does not prove buying terms, payout, call acceptance or lead consent. Product economics must eventually join accepted buyer contract/fulfilment and true cost. Until then **CAC, expected traffic, ROI and expected revenue are `null`**.
6. **Experiment design before public site investment:** An independently reviewed, genuinely distinct research/utility page can later be put into a single original owner-controlled experiment with an explicit baseline, a real observation window, source-linked conversions and a predefined stop/continue rule. The original site/SEO/brand/CRM/revenue owners continue to own their deployment and reporting. The factory cannot approve, publish, index, send, pay or recognise revenue.

### Investment committee decision states (research-only)

| State | Reason | First action |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SOURCE_REPAIR_FIRST` | Unverified locale, insufficient source links/utility, or location-swapped copy | Repair the precise evidence and utility |
| `SOURCE_REFRESH_FIRST` | Author-entered dates are outside the bounded desk window or in future | Recheck original facts, preserve actual checked dates |
| `EDITORIAL_REPAIR_FIRST` | SlopMonster identifies pattern-level writing problems | Revise without changing source facts |
| `MEASURE_FIRST_PARTY_INTENT` | Research desk is coherent; real exact-query first-party demand not established | Original SEO owner supplies an exact query and reporting window |
| `RESEARCH_COMMERCIAL_PATH` | Source/observed query are present but relevant buyer coverage guidance is absent | Original commercial owner reviews genuine distribution/economics |
| `EVALUATE_ONE_BOUNDED_EXPERIMENT` | Multiple initial sources exist; only eligible for *owner review*, not launch | Original owners evaluate one useful pilot, independently validate rights/claims |

`_executive_screen` in the **existing** `empire_os/microsite_ranked_production_queue_v4.py` is fully deterministic, auditable, and retains the original queue schema and numeric `research_priority_points` for backward compatibility. The numeric score remains research triage, never a profit forecast.

The original queue now exposes `executive_portfolio`, with **at most three distinct markets** in a research desk shortlist, and an explicit next evidence action per eligible candidate. The research shortlist has **zero permission to invest, publish, spend or send**. It is not an external campaign builder.
