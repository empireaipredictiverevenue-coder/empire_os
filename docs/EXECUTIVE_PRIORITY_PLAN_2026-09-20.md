# Empire AI — Executive Priority Plan

Date: 2026-09-20
Owner: Founder + Chief of Staff
Mode: company coordination / OBSERVE where execution is consequential

This is the current Chief-of-Staff priority stack. It deliberately limits the
company to three top-level priorities while R&D, Marketing and Engineering run
supporting work in parallel.

## Company Priority 1 — Close the First Verified Revenue Loop

Objective:
Prove the complete commercial loop with real evidence:

**buyer -> commercial terms -> verified BSC USDT payment -> fulfilment ->
outcome -> recognized revenue -> realized gross profit -> Predictive Revenue /
Astra feedback.**

Why first:
- creates commercial truth
- unlocks calibration
- validates buyer/commercial model
- supplies proof for Marketing
- supplies outcome-conditioned memory for AGI
- supplies real updates for Quant Brain
- is the gating evidence for any future Astra authority expansion

Current blockers to manage:
- verified outreach-ready buyer capacity
- buyer/commercial activation
- genuine payment/fulfilment/outcome evidence

Supporting owners:
- Buyer Commercial / Revenue
- Revenue CRM
- Conversation/Closer
- Payments
- Astra observer
- Marketing for buyer demand/proof assets

Success:
At least one independently verifiable recognized revenue event with associated
buyer, payment, fulfilment, outcome and gross-profit evidence.

No forecast/simulation counts as completion.

## Founder Attention Rules

Interrupt:
- security/legal material event
- production outage
- material payment/revenue issue
- irreversible high-risk decision

Same-day:
- live commercial approval
- critical customer/buyer issue
- launch blocker
- pricing/contract exception

Weekly:
- roadmap
- campaigns
- R&D bets
- partnerships
- resourcing

Delegate:
- everything within approved operating policy.

## Kill List

Chief of Staff should stop:
- duplicate agents solving the same workflow
- synthetic metrics presented as progress
- disconnected products with no buyer/monetization path
- campaigns without attribution
- R&D with no learning question
- work with no owner
- priorities that remain blocked without escalation
- features that do not connect to product/customer/revenue or strategic moat

## Operating Rule

**The company may have many workstreams, but only three top-level priorities.**

Astra ranks machine work.
Functional leaders run their departments.
Chief of Staff keeps the company aligned.
Founder makes the few decisions that truly require founder authority.
