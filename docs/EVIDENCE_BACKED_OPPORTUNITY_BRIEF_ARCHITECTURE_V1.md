# Evidence-Backed One-Page Opportunity Brief V1

## Purpose

Close the internal product gap between observed account intelligence and the
"send the one-page example" conversation path without inventing buyer intent,
commercial demand, results, price, capacity or revenue.

## Inputs

The first runtime producer consumes the canonical account intelligence brief
batch snapshot. Only claims whose claim critic verdict is SUPPORTED may appear
as observed facts. Search observations that remain unverified cannot be
published as facts.

## Output

Each brief contains company identity, observed supported facts with source
references, evidence quality, bounded opportunity hypotheses labelled as tests,
one recommended first test, explicit unknowns, and source references.

Review-ready means internally reviewable only. It never means send-approved.

## Authority

Mode is REVIEW_ONLY. The module cannot infer buyer intent or demand, claim
customer results or market share, accept terms, create or approve outbound,
place calls, allocate capacity, create payment requests, move funds, or
recognize revenue. External delivery remains governed by existing outbound and
closer approval paths.
