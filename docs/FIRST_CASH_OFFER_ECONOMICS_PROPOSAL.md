# Empire First-Cash Offer — Founder Economics Proposal

Date: 2026-09-21
Status: PROPOSED / NOT APPROVED / NOT SELLABLE

## Product

Canonical current offer key: `managed_service`

Commercial framing:
**Empire Opportunity Intelligence Pilot**

This is the paid step after the free evidence-backed one-page opportunity brief
already used in current outbound.

The pilot is not generic "marketing management". It is a bounded commercial
engagement in which Empire:
1. maps observed demand / market / search / competitor / event signals;
2. produces a prioritized opportunity brief;
3. monitors and refreshes the opportunity picture during the pilot;
4. identifies the highest-evidence commercial actions / territories;
5. supports a bounded managed execution handoff where agreed;
6. records buyer conversation, terms, payment, fulfilment and outcomes separately
   from forecasts and scores.

These numbers are a founder-policy proposal, not observed historical costs.

- Currency: USD
- Billing model: flat pilot
- Commercial unit: per_pilot

- Acquisition-cost policy ceiling: **$300 per pilot**
- Fulfilment-cost policy ceiling: **$300 per pilot**

The $300 acquisition and $300 fulfilment figures are policy ceilings for launch
governance, not claims that Empire has already observed those exact costs.

qualified operators to review a small pilot and the evidence behind it.

Approving this offer therefore monetizes the path already in production without
requiring the current outbound campaign to be relabelled or restarted.

The pilot is the first-cash wedge, not the whole Empire business model.
