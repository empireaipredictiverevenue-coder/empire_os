# National Storm Hub — Product truth

An Empire AI-operated consumer-facing storm intelligence and preparedness website, not affiliated with NWS/NOAA. Current stage: public early release.

## People and purposes
- US homeowners/renters need calm, credible official weather alerts by ZIP/state, preparedness steps and recovery guidance, often on a phone under stress.
- Founder needs a separate protected view of existing EmpireOS company-wide Revenue Pulse, never falsely credited to National Storm Hub.
- Contractors: future separately verified optional service, not a live marketplace.

## Current functions
Home, Atlas, Alerts Centre, Storm Predictor, source library, recovery, Texas and Florida hubs, browser-local Passport checklist/bookmarks/export, private founder read model.

## Truth and safety
Forecast != observed storm != property damage; empty NWS response != all-clear; network failure != no risk. Browser popups function only while page is open, not as reliable emergency push. Passport is browser-local, not a secure cloud property vault. Do not infer buyer demand or earned revenue from weather alerts. No paid traffic/editorial or unapproved outreach. Preserve the live site and use guarded releases.
