### Owner's visual direction
- **Emergency colors:** true near-black, vivid controlled red, white reading surfaces, bright safety yellow.
- **Maintain intelligence/premium:** restrained use of red; typographic precision; deliberate whitespace; legible data; weather-truth hierarchy; layered atmospheric hero.
- Red is used for branded decisive action and issued severe warnings, not as an invented risk claim. Yellow flags "caution / pay attention"; white is text and neutral data.
- Existing conceptual globe stays labelled illustration, not an official 3D storm map. No fake radar/forecast, copied 3D assets or unauthorized third-party imagery.

## Design directions compared BEFORE making a production change

| Attribute | Signal Noir (default candidate) | Command Red (alternative) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Target audience | General homeowners + premium intelligence | More broadcast/control-room leaning |
| Primary canvas | Graphite black + floating restrained crimson atmosphere | Near-black + sharper red structural linework |
| Red proportion | Concentrated CTAs / title / active alerts | More prominent in edge accents / lighting |
| Yellow | Source-timestamp highlights, caution notices | More frequent in badges and caution rails |
| Readability | White and silver-white content, discreet data frames | White and silver-white with high red emphasis |
| Converting visitor | One ZIP form + Alerts and Predictor | Same real functions, unchanged data |
| Risk | Might feel insufficiently urgent | Risks feeling like an alarm site if overused |
| Evaluation | 1440, 820, 390, 320; screenshot and keyboard | Same; confirm no false red when no weather risk |

**Default recommendation:** Signal Noir. Command Red remains a private comparison, not a separate deployed product.
