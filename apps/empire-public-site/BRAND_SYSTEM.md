# Empire AI Brand System

## Core idea
The Empire mark represents a predictive core: a central intelligence signal surrounded by forward-cut rails that form an abstract E. The outer hexagonal frame suggests infrastructure, control and governed execution.

## Primary assets
- `public/brand/empire-mark.svg` — primary standalone mark
- `public/brand/empire-logo.svg` — horizontal mark + wordmark lockup
- `public/brand/empire-mark-mono.svg` — one-colour mark for embossing, print, legal and restricted surfaces
- `src/app/icon.svg` — browser/app icon derived from the primary mark

## Colour system

## Usage
Keep clear space around the mark equal to at least 18% of the mark width. Do not stretch, skew, rotate, add unapproved gradients or place the full-colour mark over visually noisy imagery.

At small sizes use the standalone mark only. Use the horizontal lockup when width allows. Use the mono mark when colour reproduction is constrained.

## Motion direction
For motion branding, animate the central core pulse first, then the signal spine, then the outer frame. Avoid spinning the full logo continuously; the identity should feel controlled rather than decorative.
