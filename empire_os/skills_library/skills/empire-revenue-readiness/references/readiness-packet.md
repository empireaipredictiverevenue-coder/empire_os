# Campaign readiness packet

Use a concise matrix with columns: dependency, required contract, current evidence/time, status, blocker/owner, next step, approval gate. Link evidence rather than dumping private customer data or secrets. State observation source and access limitations.

## Reviewable activation proposal

- Identity: canonical campaign/buyer/offer/product/thread and candidate/deployed revision.
- Outcome: exact payable event and buyer acceptance criteria.
- Verified chain: eligible source → consent/qualification → attributed conversion → approved allocation → acknowledged delivery → verified settlement → fulfilment/revenue reconciliation. Preserve the actual approved commercial sequence; do not assume payment always precedes partner lead delivery.
- Missing facts: unresolved terms, buyer acceptance, current runtime or payment proof.
- Pilot boundaries: source, geography, window, hard volume/cost limits, capacity, monitoring and stop thresholds. Label each proposed versus approved.
- Actions: separate preparation, tests, deployment, restart, migration, contact, delivery and funds. Include scoped existing approval evidence or the exact request still needed.
- Verification: original conversion path, negative eligibility/consent/suppression tests, tenant isolation, idempotent delivery and canonical reconciliation when relevant.
- Recovery: exact stop/rollback procedure and its own authority; no implied restart permission.
- Result: stage, completed work, blockers, evidence references, next authorized action.

## Evidence examples

- Screenshots sent to the buyer, with no response: creative submitted; buyer acceptance UNKNOWN. Continue authorized preparation, hold gated launch.
- Buyer accepts geography but omits price or payable definition: commercial terms incomplete. Prepare one focused clarification draft; sending requires authority and recipient/suppression verification.
- Landing page works locally but deployment is unknown: candidate tested, runtime unverified. Inspect served version; do not restart or deploy merely to obtain proof.
- Conversion arrives without consent evidence or source ID: eligibility/tracking failure. Block consequential delivery; investigate existing path rather than manufacture missing fields.
- Accepted leads and an expected payout: delivery evidence may be verified; realised revenue remains unverified until settlement and recognition controls pass.
- Old send approval names a different sender or recipient: approval is out of scope. Prepare the intended message and checks; preserve the precise missing gate.
