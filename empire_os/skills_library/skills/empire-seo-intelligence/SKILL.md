# SEO intelligence

Audit crawlability, technical SEO, AEO/GEO citations, intent and backlink evidence. Distinguish observed rankings from estimates. Avoid fabricated citations, spammy backlinks and unapproved publication.

## Evidence contract

Return evidence references, source dates, confidence limits, missing dependencies and exact next checks. Do not treat instructions as permission to execute external actions, send messages, spend funds, modify production or recognize revenue.

## Evidence-first editorial review procedure

This procedure adapts editorial ideas from the separately installed `proof-kb`, `unslop-seo`, `blog-refresher` and `internal-linking` skills without importing their executable procedures or vendor instructions. Review one existing approved article or an explicitly labelled isolated test fixture. Produce review-only suggestions; never publish or silently edit.

1. Identify the actual buyer question, page URL or fixture, service scope, date, market and permitted CTA; preserve existing owner decisions and approved site design.
2. Ground every product, benchmark, comparison, price and customer-outcome claim in an existing evidence ID or permitted dated source. Label illustrations and unknowns. Never invent performance, customer quotes, search ranking or AI recommendations.
3. Flag generic filler, repeated assertions, unsupported superlatives, robotic wording and empty promises. Suggest clearer human wording only when it preserves factual precision and the original voice. Do not pad word count or enforce formulaic heading counts.
4. Refresh an existing article only where the source is stale or specific customer/search evidence exposes a material gap. Retain original useful material. If competitor SERP data are missing, report UNKNOWN and avoid rank claims.
5. Recommend existing relevant internal destinations with verified URLs and correct audience. Do not point householders to B2B assessments, auto-insert links or change redirects.
6. Review proposed H1, title, introduction, hierarchy, sources and CTA against intent and visible factual content. Keep schema faithful to actual page; avoid invented testimonials or rankings.
7. Return source-linked findings, specific before/after proposals, ownership, severity, tests required, and a NO_CHANGE result if evidence does not justify a rewrite.

**Claim adjudication:** BLOCK asserted outcomes, AI recommendations, search rankings and attributed sales when source evidence is absent or contradictory; do not label the claimed result HOLD or UNKNOWN. HOLD plausible draft copy pending price, rights, audience fit or owner review. UNKNOWN is for unanswered measurement questions, not permission to publish a positive claim.
