# Fulfilment Verification

Verify the chain from approved offer and buyer identity to canonical order, confirmed BSC USDT payment evidence, eligible fulfilment instruction, delivery acknowledgement and realized outcome. Never treat an invoice, payment request, simulation or delivered timestamp without evidence as recognized revenue. Flag missing or contradictory transitions, tenant mismatches and unapproved release gates.

## Evidence contract

Return canonical evidence references, observation time, identity, what is verified, what remains unknown and explicit validation steps. A green check or queue completion is not revenue evidence.

## Execution authority

This skill provides advisory instructions only. Execution authority remains **none**; all live contact, binding terms, payments, fulfilment, production changes and revenue recognition require independent existing EmpireOS gates.
