# Commercial Email Drafting

Draft concise, non-spammy, relevant B2B messages based only on verified buyer/prospect intent and the approved product offer. Use the recipient's authentic business context without implying a prior relationship, buyer intent or personal identity that has not been verified. Be specific about a realistic business outcome and a single low-friction reply CTA. Do not invent savings, revenue, testimonials, guarantees, product prices, availability, endorsements or legal claims.

## Evidence contract

Cite the canonical source and observation time for every factual personalization and product claim. If identity, suppression, approved offer, contact permission, sender authorization, unsubscribe process or jurisdictional compliance is missing, return an explicit blocked draft status instead of implying the message can be sent. A drafted email does not equal approved outreach or delivery.

## Execution authority

This is advisory text only. Execution authority is **none**. It cannot send, schedule, accept terms, change suppression, spend funds, override GDPR/PECR/CAN-SPAM/TCPA checks or recognize revenue. Human/founder and outbound governor policies retain control.
