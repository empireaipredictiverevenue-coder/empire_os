# Empire AI — Scientific Evidence & Reproducibility

This skill guides **the existing Scientific Agent and Data Brokerage research owner**. It is not a second research agent, scraper, model, product, skill registry, scheduler or authorisation mechanism. Use for scientific papers, laboratory outcomes, experiments, technology claims, patents, scientific datasets, and evidence-backed commercial intelligence.

## Retrieve, scope and preserve

Recover the original question, prior study/blueprint, researcher or buyer's *actual* decision, signed source receipts, original source owner, previous evidence, licences, rejected claims, and current status. Separate discovery, scientific evidence, technology readiness, buyer demand and customer-delivery authority. Form a precise, falsifiable question rather than starting broad unbounded collection. Never fabricate results or silently replace an original report.

## Original sources and rights

Record for each publication: DOI, first-party source/publisher URL, authors where independently evidenced, journal/proceedings/review/preprint classification, publication and observation dates, corrections/retraction notices, and source version.

Crossref and OpenAlex can match DOI/title metadata; **OpenAlex can ingest Crossref**, so an index match is not experimental corroboration or proof of independent peer review. Check duplicate-title DOIs, missing records, reviews versus experiments, meeting abstracts, correspondence, editorials, conflicting versions and retractions. Hold unresolved matches.

Field-level rights are separate: metadata, author abstracts, images, full text, supplemental datasets, figures, patents, private patient or customer records, and machine-learning rights may differ. Open metadata does not automatically licence publisher full texts, third-party abstracts, data redistribution or customer sale. Preserve `RESEARCH_ONLY` until explicit licensing and original owner approval.

## Evaluate the experiment, not its headline

For each primary study create a reproducible evidence sheet:

1. The authors' **precise claim** and material, system or population; distinguish experimental result, simulation, review or marketing claim.
2. Controls, sample size, independent baselines, replicates, statistical uncertainty, error bars, measurement instruments, operating conditions, data/code access and publication disclosures.
3. Record limitations, funding, conflicts of interest, contradictory results and independent studies. A citation count, DOI or publisher-asserted peer review does not prove independent replication.
4. For a proposed replication, specify preregistered hypothesis, independent measurement, control/treatment, observable outcome, acceptance threshold, negative controls, time window and stop conditions. Never present a developer-authored **synthetic world model** as validated real-world science or predictive calibration.
5. Evaluate transfer to real-world use separately: manufacturing yield, safety, economic scale, degradation, regulation, IP and freedom to operate. Do not infer customer demand, a purchase order or scientific commercial success from laboratory performance.

For **silicon-anode batteries**, normalise full-cell/half-cell, cell format, cathode, C-rate, loading, temperature, swelling, initial coulombic efficiency, N/P ratio, volumetric/gravimetric capacity, cycle-life definition and scale-up before comparing outcomes. For **perovskite solar**, distinguish cell/module area, independently certified efficiency, degradation and outdoor life. For **carbon capture**, distinguish measured capture energy, material inputs, life-cycle net effect, permanence and deployed scale.

## Evidence stages and failure modes

- `METADATA_MATCHED`: verified catalogue identifier/title match, not independent research confirmation.
- `PUBLISHER_METHODS_REVIEWED`: original paper methods/results/disclosures checked; outcomes still attributed to its authors.
- `INDEPENDENT_LAB_REPLICATION_VERIFIED`: a separately identified independent group reproduced a comparable controlled outcome.
- `TECHNOLOGY_READINESS_VERIFIED`: credible independent field/manufacturing evidence supports the *stated* readiness definition.
- `BUYER_DEMAND_VERIFIED`: an authentic buyer requests research or confirms a genuine purchasing requirement, not merely a matching portfolio.
- `RIGHTS_CLEARED_FOR_DEFINED_DELIVERY`: rights and permitted output confirmed for exact fields, scope and recipient.

Unknown, unsigned, stale, inaccessible, inconsistent, licence-ineligible or contradictory evidence stays `UNKNOWN` or `HOLD`. Missing proof is not a zero-risk finding. Separate author claims from Empire AI verification.

## Existing commercial handoff

Reuse **`founder_original_intelligence_reports`**, existing Data Brokerage source receipts, Search Fabric, Buyer Scout, Smart CRM, Sales Director and founder-approved outbound governor. A research report should be concise and helpful to a buyer's *verified* job, explain strength and contradictions, preserve original source links and date, explicitly distinguish non-primary reviews, document unanswered diligence questions and state where a specialist human review is still needed.

A first contact must be relationship-first and must pass existing contact-history, recipient identity, suppression and permission controls. A helpful observation is not a purchasing signal. Do not claim revenue without approved delivery, accepted contract, verified payment and recognition.

## Evidence contract

Return **original source**, dated observation, methods and conditions, study classification, author claim, independent validation status, contradictory evidence, risks, rights, buyer demand, owner, next bounded test and current gate. Include signed receipt/version/hash when available. Distinguish `implemented`, `tested`, `independently verified`, `founder-approved`, `live`, `delivered` and `paid`. Use no invented confidence score, citation count, independent replication, licence, customer or revenue.
