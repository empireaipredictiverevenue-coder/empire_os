## Performance Metrics

### Campaign KPIs
- **Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)**: Revenue generated per advertising dollar
- **Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)**: Cost to acquire a qualified lead
- **Conversion Rate**: Percentage of leads that become customers
- **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)**: Total revenue from acquired customers
- **Revenue Attribution**: Full tracking from initial engagement to closed deal

### Operations KPIs
- **Campaign Response Time**: Time from launch to first results
- **Optimization Frequency**: How often campaigns are adjusted
- **Automation Coverage**: Percentage of processes automated
- **Data Quality**: Accuracy and completeness of tracking data
